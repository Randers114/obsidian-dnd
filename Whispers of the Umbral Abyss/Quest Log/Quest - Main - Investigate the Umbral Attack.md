# Objective
- Investigate the coordinated attacks, abductions, and disappearances
- Uncover the purpose of the ritual involving livestock and Aether
- Identify the key figures behind the operation
- Stop or disrupt the ritual at [[Silvercut Crossroads]]
- Find the captives in [[Thortil]]
- Escort captives from [[Thortil]] to safety
- Follow the trail south to locate remaining captives

---

# Background
- The city of [[Kymal]] was attacked by coordinated forces led by a vampire-like figure
- Attackers captured people in the city, but livestock in surrounding farms
- The attackers mentioned a **ritual** and were searching for something at [[The Fleeting Pages]]
- The book [[On the edge of the Abyss]] was targeted but is missing
- A symbol (half-moon with an eye) links multiple attack sites
- [[Brynja Ironbrow]] connected the attacks to disappearances at nearby farms
- At [[Tallstack Farm]], livestock was taken while humans were deliberately ignored
- Markings associated with the attackers appear *after* the attacks
- The cult refers to itself as the [[Sect of The Writhing Warden]]
- Aether is being harvested and used as part of the ritual process
- The ritual is tied to the timing of a **Blood Moon**
- [[The Writhing Warden]] has been identified as an ancient demon lord that was imprisoned long ago
- The cult’s ritual may be aimed at releasing or empowering this entity *(uncertain)*
- Aether is being actively produced through experimentation within cult-controlled sites
- The cult is connected to a vampire organization known as [[The Umbral Abyss]]
- Aether grants increased power when consumed but drains life force
- In large quantities, Aether can be used to free or summon demon lords
