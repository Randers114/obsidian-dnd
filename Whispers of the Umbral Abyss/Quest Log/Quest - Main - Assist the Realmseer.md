# Objective
- Assist [[Eskyld]] in completing his portal experiment
- Acquire the required components:
  - Residuum from [[Whitestone]]
  - Root of the Suntree from [[Stillben]]
  - Divine tear from [[Shadebarrow]]

---

# Background
- [[Eskyld]], the Realmseer, was trapped due to a failed experiment involving portal magic
- His goal is to open a portal to [[Siegel]], a central point in the multiverse
- He requires three rare and controversial components to complete the experiment
- His previous organization, [[Arcana Pansophical]], rejected his work
