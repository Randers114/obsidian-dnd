# Origins 
[[Tal'Dorei]]
[[Rexxentrum]]
[[Wildemount]]
[[Kaelos]] was abandoned as a child and does not remember much about his parents, they were both Tieflings. At a young age he was waiting for his parents to come home and they never did, later the landlord threw Kaelos out on the street. He remembers his dad [[Morros]] and his mom [[Lelith]] as a bit distant but not as bad parents, and he does not know what happened to them. 

He grew up in the city of [[Rexxentrum]] in [[Wildemount]], first with his parents and then later on the street. He did odd jobs here and there for money and food, when that was not enough and he grew hungry he had to steal food. After living like this for a number of years he met [[Haldon]] and [[Faelen]], two other orphans living on the street. They brought him back to their group and he was welcomed into the group. The group leader was [[Niel]], he was a bit older than the others and they looked up to him. He was the one who planned how they would get food and found them either small jobs or targets for stealing. They were 6 in the group: [[Niel]], [[Haldon]], [[Faelen]], [[Half Elf Name]], [[Female Name 1]], [[Female Name 2]]. 

They spent years living on the streets together. [[Kaelos]] was always curious about the wider world, he wanted to see more than just the dirty city streets where they were living. Whenever he could convince the group they hitched rides in caravans between nearby cities. Mostly in exchange for serving the caravan in the duration. [[Niel]] was never happy with switching cities as he had all his knowledge in [[Rexxentrum]].  

One day [[Niel]] came running back to where [[Kaelos]] was sleeping, he was alone in their sleeping camp to guard the few possessions they had while the others were out finding food. He woke the half sleeping [[Kaelos]] telling him to run, he said that they were attacked by a bandit group they tried stealing from and that [[Half Elf Name]] and [[Haldon]] were dead, the others scattered and that [[Kaelos]] should flee the city, and that they could find each other later. 

[[Kaelos]] ran into the forest outside the [[Druvenlode]] in a panic and kept running until he could no more, he slumped and slept in the forest. He wanted to go back to [[Rexxentrum]] to meet up with the others as they would surely be there. 

He grew up on the streets of cities sometimes hitching rides between them. One city in particular Kaelos remembers because of a few friends he made as a child. After they went missing he decided to leave and find them or clues as to where they were. But years have passed and he has almost given up hope of finding information about them. 

He always loved to discover new places and moved a lot in the wilderness and forests. He decided to move out and live in the wild for some time to build a home which he never had. That is when the slavers found his home, burned it down and took him prisoner. He was held for years doing things for them. After one day of particular brutal punishment for not fulfilling the duties he had to their satisfaction he was offered power by a mysterious entity. It has to be benevolent as it helped him in his time of need. He accepted the power. When one of the slavers came for another round of punishment he fought his way free, hurt or killed the man, he does not know as he fled into the night. 

After that ordeal he was determined to get as far away from where he knew they were operating and only come back when he knows that he can do something to stop them.
