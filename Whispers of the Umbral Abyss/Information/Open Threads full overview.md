# Open Threads

A living overview of unresolved plot threads in **Whispers of the Umbral Abyss**.

## How to read this note
- **Active now** = immediate priorities
- **Ongoing** = important unresolved threads still likely to matter soon
- **Dormant** = unresolved, but not currently urgent
- **Personal** = threads tied closely to party members
- **Uncertain** = strong possibilities or suspicions, but not confirmed facts

# Active Now

## [[Quest - Main - Assist the Realmseer]]
**Status:** Active  
**Priority:** Main

The party is currently inside [[Shadebarrow]] searching for the **Divine Tear**, the final known component needed for [[Eskyld]]’s portal experiment.

### What we know
- [[Eskyld]] needs:
  - Residuum from [[Whitestone]]
  - Root of the [[Suntree]]
  - Divine Tear from [[Shadebarrow]]
- The party already secured the root and enough residuum for the current stage.
- [[Shadebarrow]] is an old fortress associated with the Betrayer Gods.
- Entry deeper into the fortress appears tied to sacrifice, faith, or divine recognition.
- [[Roland]] already sacrificed a childhood holy symbol of [[The Dawnfather]] to continue deeper.

### Open questions
- Where exactly is the Divine Tear?
- What further tests or sacrifices will [[Shadebarrow]] demand?
- Why was [[Avandra]]’s symbol scratched out?
- What consequences will there be for taking the Divine Tear?
- Is [[Eskyld]] fully right about what this experiment will do?
