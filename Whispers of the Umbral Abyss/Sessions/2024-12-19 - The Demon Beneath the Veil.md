## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – Knowledge in Westruun
While in [[Westruun]], the group finds a notice from [[Jekt]], assistant to the Realmseer, requesting help investigating the Realmseer’s disappearance and asking for a meeting at [[Slaterock Tower]].

At the [[Cobalt Soul]], [[Maren]] directs them to archivist [[Lorvein Drenner]], who helps them locate information about [[The Writhing Warden]].

They learn that the entity is an **ancient demon lord**, known for devouring other beings to increase its power, and that it was imprisoned long ago.

They also uncover a symbol associated with the ruling family of [[Westruun]] that resembles markings found in earlier investigations, suggesting a possible connection *(uncertain)*.

---

### Scene 2 – Journey to Thortil
The group decides to prioritize stopping the ritual and travels to [[Thortil]].

Outside the city, they discover an obsidian pillar bearing the inscription:
> “To the death of [[Warren Drassic]]”

The meaning of this is unclear.

As they approach, they notice activity within the city and move stealthily closer.

---

### Scene 3 – The Withered Chapel
Inside [[Thortil]], the group identifies a chapel dedicated to the Dawnfather as the center of activity.

The surrounding environment is unnaturally corrupted:
- Trees around the chapel are dead
- The area feels tainted

They locate stairs leading into a crypt beneath the chapel and descend.

Below, they encounter and fight a water elemental, suggesting magical or ritual defenses protecting the area.
