# 2025-04-03 - The Automaton and the Three Prisms

# To do

## Recap 


## Summary 
* Roland and Kaelos explore a maze, finding a door with a bull's head engraving.
* They discover a prison area and reactivate a mechanical automaton named C.H.A.R.L.E.S.
* C.H.A.R.L.E.S. joins the party as they continue through the maze.
* They encounter traps, including grabbing hands that restrain Roland.
* The party collects gemstones from the labyrinth and uses them to open the bull's head door.
* After defeating a minotaur, they retrieve the third prism from the bull's head.
* Returning to the tower, Kaelos assembles the three prisms.
* The prisms implode, revealing a man with a white beard who thanks the party for their arrival.

## Story 
After walking through the maze more [[Roland]] and [[Kaelos]] find a door with an engraving of a bulls head. The door does not seem magical but it seems like it might need a mast of a bulls head, they keep looking through the maze. They find what seems to be a prison area with chains and dried blood, in one of the rooms they find a metal man that seems mechanical with a cape. They decide to reactivate the automaton and he starts speaking to them. He tells them that he has been on the run for as long as he can remember, and he went through a portal and cannot remember any more. [[Kaelos]] frees the automaton from the chains and he says that his name is [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]]. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] joins them in going through the maze and at the next dead end they see a flicker of the three of them together. Afraid that this could be another mimic they decide to move on, at the next dead hands shoot out from the wall and grabs at them. The hands get ahold of [[Roland]] and restrains him. They destroy the hands, and move further in the labyrinth, they find another gemstone and when [[Kaelos]] lifts it from the pedestal they hear a mechanism sound from back towards where they came from. They go back and find the door with the bulls head engraving open. 

Inside they see a minotaur and it runs towards them with a great axe. After they beat the minotaur, they see a giant bulls head in stone. In front of the head there seems to be indents that has the same size as the gemstones they found around the labyrinth. They roll in the gemstones and then the bull head opens it mouth, here they find the third prism. A door opens in the wall and reveal the portal they entered the labyrinth through. They step through and emerges back in the tower with the 3 portals. 

[[Kaelos]] assembles the 3 prisms and a sound emerges from the prisms as they start to spin. It then implodes and they hear a scream of pain from a man as he expands from the implosion of the prism. A man is standing where the prisms where with a cape and a white beard. He stops screaming and say "Thank god you came".
