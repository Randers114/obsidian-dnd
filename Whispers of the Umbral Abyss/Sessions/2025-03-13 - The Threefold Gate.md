## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – Knowledge of the Abyss
The group, now calling themselves the [[Wayfarers Accord]], descends into the archives of the [[Cobalt Soul]] with [[Lorvein Drenner]].

While they find no direct information about [[The Umbral Abyss]], they uncover knowledge about **The Abyss** itself:
- A chaotic plane with many layers
- Each layer ruled by a demon lord
- Known domains include:
  - Yeenoghu (The Rotted Den)
  - Demogorgon (The Gaping Maw)
  - Orcus (Thanatos)
  - Zuggtmoy (The Screaming Pit)
  - Baphomet (The Endless Maze)

They also learn:
- Most Abyssal portals are **one-way**

---

### Scene 2 – Slaterock Tower
The group travels to [[Slaterock Tower]] to follow up on the disappearance of the Realmseer.

Inside, the tower is eerily silent and appears abandoned.

As they ascend, they hear a faint voice echoing through the structure:
> *"help"*

At the top, they discover three portals and hear a voice command:
> *"split"*

---

### Scene 3 – The Three Paths
Each portal reveals a different scene:
- A bright open platform beneath a blue sky
- A dark, damp crypt
- Complete darkness

Nearby, [[Roland]] discovers notes referencing:
- [[Ayor]]
- [[Avalir]]
- [[Zemniaz]]
- An unnamed city that crashed in [[Thaldory]]

---

### Scene 4 – The First Portal
The group chooses the first portal.

They emerge onto a **marble platform high in the sky**, surrounded by intense winds. The ground below is not visible.

After solving a puzzle, metal doors open, revealing another challenge ahead.
