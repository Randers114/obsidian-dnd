## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The Letter Revealed
The group searches the exterior of the chapel but finds nothing further. They return to [[Tallstack Farm]] and are welcomed by [[Joren]] and his family. After sharing a meal, they retire to the guest house to examine the letter recovered earlier.

Using magic, [[Kaelos]] translates the letter. It reveals a coordinated plan involving livestock sacrifice, the harvesting of “Aether,” and a ritual to take place at [[Silvercut Crossroads]] under the Blood Moon. The letter is signed by [[Riven Malakar]] and addressed to [[Mavrik]], with instructions to meet [[Zevrin]].

---

### Scene 2 – The False Trail
The next day, the group follows the more obvious tracks. They arrive at a hill where dragging marks lead upward toward a cart.

They are ambushed by cultists. During the fight, one shouts that [[The Writhing Warden]] will not let them escape.

After defeating the group and capturing the leader, they interrogate him, but he reveals little beyond repeating warnings about the rise of [[The Writhing Warden]]. They execute him and discover a second note indicating the trail was deliberately staged to mislead them.

---

### Scene 3 – Return to Kymal
The group briefly returns to [[Tallstack Farm]] to inform [[Joren]] before heading back to [[Kymal]]. At the courthouse, they meet [[Brynja Ironbrow]].

She shares that during the attack, a figure transformed into a bat, and recalls that the region was once home to vampire families centuries ago. She suggests the group seek knowledge from the [[Cobalt Soul]].

---

### Scene 4 – Silvercut Crossroads
Instead, the group travels to [[Silvercut Crossroads]]. The area is eerily quiet, centered around a monument bearing a message about justice and the innocent dead.

While investigating the ruins, they are attacked by undead and later a ghost.

Continuing their search, they discover blood droplets leading into a house. Inside, they find a hatch leading down into a deep crypt.

Below, they find rows of ancient coffins and follow a set of footprints into a larger chamber. There, a man stands among walls lined with slaughtered animals hanging from hooks. The same symbol seen at the farm and chapel is present.
