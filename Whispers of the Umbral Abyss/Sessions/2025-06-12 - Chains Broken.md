## Recap 


## Summary 

- After defeating the slavers, the group finds a key, treasures (including platinum, gold, silver, gemstones, paintings, and statues), and a letter from [[Valerya Noir]], the Ringmaster of the [[Midnight Managerie]], ordering the capture of exotic specimens for a showcase.
- Deeper in the cave, they rescue more prisoners and discover a shrine to [[Gruumsh The Ruiner]] with a ring on its altar. No further valuables are found, and they decide to escort the prisoners out.
- While attempting to return to [[Sephrah]], the group gets lost and camps in a cave. During the night, four of the eight prisoners die from injuries, and the group constructs a harness to carry them.
- They return to the monastery, leave the surviving and deceased prisoners with the monks, then head to the city to speak with [[Leada]].
- [[Leada]] directs them to the tavern [[The Breezy Plateau]] and mentions sightings of elementals near the [[Plane of Air]] portal. She suggests they speak to her sister [[Maeve]] if they choose to help.
- The group rests at the tavern with innkeeper [[Tariic]], informs [[Leada]] they have business in [[Stillben]] first, and departs. On the way, they camp near a swamp, fight a swamp monster, and arrive at [[Stillben]].

## Story 

After beating all the slavers they investigate them to see if they have any info on them about the group. On what seems like the boss they find a key. They find the tent of the leader where they find 34 platinum, 327 gold, 213 silver, bag of gemstones (2/3s diamonds), many paintings and statues. They also find a letter which [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] reads aloud. It is about orders from the [[Midnight Managerie]], they decree that they should find specimens of exotic races for their upcoming showcase. It is signed [[Valerya Noir]] who is the Ringmaster of the [[Midnight Managerie]]. 

They keep going into the cave where they free more prisoners and find more living space for the orcs. They also find a shrine to a god, it is [[Gruumsh The Ruiner]] on the alter they find a ring. They search the living space but do not find anything of value. They decide to go out to the freed prisoners. The group leaves the camp with the prisoners and decide to go back to the city [[Sephrah]] to find [[Leada]]. They get lost on the way and end up back at the camp, it was too risky to enter the camp again and they decide to see if they can find a cave to spend the night. They find the cave and set up camp for the night. 4 of the 8 freed prisoners succumb to their injuries during the night and the group fashions a harness to drag them back from a tent. 

The next day, they go back towards the city again. They find their way back to the Monastery. They leave the freed prisoners with the monks and give them the dead people as well. The group go to the city to find [[Leada]]. [[Leada]] points the group towards an tavern in the city [[The Breezy Plateau]] . She also says that she could not offer much for the job the group did for the kidnapped people. She also says that they could use some help with another job, there has been seen larger groups of elementals nearby in the portal to the [[Plane of Air]].  She also says to start this we need to talk to her sister [[Maeve]]. 

They go to the tavern and get some rooms and something to eat. They are met by the tavern keeper [[Tariic]] (half elf). The group inform [[Leada]] that they have another job in [[Stillben]] that they need to do first, but they will come back after to take the job if it is still available. They leave the city and travel towards [[Stillben]]. 

They arrive at a swamp and decide to make camp before continuing. Next morning they enter the swamp and halfway through they are attacked by a swamp monster. They defeat the monster and continue towards the city and arrive at [[Stillben]].
