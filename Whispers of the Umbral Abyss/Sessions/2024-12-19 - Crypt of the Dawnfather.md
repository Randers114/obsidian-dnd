## Summary 
- On a noticeboard in the city, the group finds a letter from [[Jekt]], the assistant to the Realmseer, asking them to investigate the Realmseer's disappearance and to meet at the [[Slaterock Tower]].
- After reading the notice, the group heads to the [[Cobalt Soul]], where the librarian [[Maren]] advises them to contact archivist [[Lorvein Drenner]].
- The group meets [[Lorvein Drenner]], who guides them to library sections containing information about the cult of [[The Writhing Warden]].
- They learn that [[The Writhing Warden]] is an ancient demon lord known for devouring entities to fuel his power and was imprisoned long ago.
- The group also discovers a family symbol for the new leader of [[Westrun]] that resembles the symbol found in the church.
- With little additional information available, they decide to travel to [[Thortil]] to stop the ritual aimed at extracting Aether from captive people from [[Kymal]].
- Outside [[Thortil]], they find an obsidian pillar with a plaque reading "To the death of [[Warren Drassic]]".
- Observing lights and activity from inside the city, the group sneaks closer and finds a chapel to the dawnfather, where all the surrounding trees are dead.
- Outside the chapel, they discover a set of stairs leading into a crypt below.
- In the crypt, the group descends and engages in a battle with a water elemental.

## Story 

On a noticeboard in the city the group finds a letter from [[Jekt]], the assistant to the Realmseer. In the note he asks to investigate the disappearance of the Realmseer and to meet him at the [[Slaterock Tower]]. After the noticeboard the group arrives at the [[Cobalt Soul]], and here the librarian [[Maren]] says that they should grab a hold of archivist [[Lorvein Drenner]]. The group goes to meet her, she leads them to the sections in the library that could hold information about the cult of [[The Writhing Warden]]. [[The Writhing Warden]] is an ancient demon lord that is known for devouring entities to fuel his power and he was imprisoned long ago. 

They also find a family symbol for the new leader of [[Westruun]] which could look like the symbol which they found in the church. The group does not find any more information and decides to travel to [[Thortil]] to stop the ritual to extract Aether from the captive people from [[Kymal]]. Outside [[Thortil]] they find an obsidian pillar with a plaque that reads "To the death of [[Warren Drassic]]". While outside they see some lights and activity from inside the city and decide to sneak closer, inside the city they find a chapel to the dawnfather that seems to be the center of the activity. Around the chapel all the trees are dead. 

The group finds some stairs outside the chapel and decides to go into the crypt outside, this leads below the chapel and below they fight a water elemental.
