The group [[Wayfarers Accord]] goes down to the archive with [[Lorvein Drenner]] to find more information about the [[The Umbral Abyss]]. Looking in the archive she does not find any more information specifically about [[The Umbral Abyss]]. About [[The Abyss]] they she finds that it is a chaotic place and it has many layers, each layer has its own demon lord. Looking more for information about demon lords they find more information about the demon lords and the layers they rule. 
"
**Known Layers of the Abyss**
    - Some layers are **well-documented** due to past encounters with fiends:
        - **The Rotted Den** – Home of **Yeenoghu**, the Gnoll Lord of Slaughter and Hunger. A realm of endless **bloodstained savannahs** and **hunting grounds**.
        - **The Gaping Maw** – **Demogorgon’s** realm, an **endless ocean** with jagged cliffs, islands of madness, and **massive, tentacled monstrosities**.

        - **The Screaming Pit** – **Zuggtmoy’s fungal nightmare**, a twisted underworld where spores replace the air, growing over everything in sight.
        - **The Endless Maze** – **Baphomet’s** lair, a colossal labyrinth of **bone and iron**, with no escape for those who enter.
        - **The Slithering Realm** – A place rumored to be controlled by **some great eldritch horror**, where even demons fear to tread.
