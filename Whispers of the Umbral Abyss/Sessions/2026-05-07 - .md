## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

The group checks to see if they have enough rope to tie together to crawl down the shaft. They tie the ropes to the chains that lie on the floor. In order to get some more length as the shaft is quite deep. With the 2 50 foot ropes and a length of the chain that can reach the edge of the shaft. They can just barely reach the bottom of the shaft. 

[[Kaelos]] is the first one to climb down the shaft, after a bit of a struggle he succeeds in climbing down the rope. When reaching the end he seers a giant statue bound and covered in chains. Around the statue three giant arms sticks out of the ground. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] is the next to climb down. While climbing down he heard screams coming from below that increased in intensity when reaching the bottom. They seem to have a mental effect but [[Kaelos]] manages to resist it. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] also manages to resist the screams. [[Roland]] starts his climb and manages to climb down the shaft. The screams affect [[Roland]] and he is easily irritable, and reacts harshly to the group when coming down. 

The group moves around the statue and on the stomach of the statue there is a large double doors. While moving around [[Roland]] manages to shake off the effect of the screams. There are three paths leading away from the room with the giant statue. The group do a shallow scout of each, one seems to be some stairs going back to the top. One leads into some raw cavern paths. After a few turns it splits out into three narrow paths, one with red light, one with violet light and the middle with magical darkness that [[Kaelos]] darkvision can't see through. They go down the final passage and enter another large room with a statue. This one has wings and a wand floating in the air in front of the statue. It is in the middle of a fountain of red fluid. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] looks down into the fluid and becomes obsessed and incapacitated by the fluid. [[Roland]] recognizes the statue as being [[Zerzus]]. [[Kaelos]] tries pushing [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] a bit but he just keeps staring at the red fluid. [[Roland]] punches [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] and he wakes from his souper. [[Roland]] mentions that [[Zerzus]] is known as the first knight and the right hand of lord of the hills. [[Roland]] grabs the floating wand, at the top of the wand there is a red face in a gem. The wand speaks inside the mind of [[Roland]] and says they should take over the world in the name of the lord of the nine hells, [[Azmodeos]] himself. The wand seems to be sentient. [[Roland]] asks who the wand is, and it responds saying it name is [[Xartaza]]. It tries again to convince [[Roland]] to take over the world and promises him power and wealth. [[Roland]] identifies the wand using a spell and it is called "The conduit of lies". He hands it to [[Kaelos]] as it seems to be a wand specialized to warlocks. Through the room is another corridor with a door. The door is locked magically but the group uses their Chime of Opening it has 9 charges left. The door opens into an enormous room with a 50ft high ceiling. Inside there is a giant pool of blood where two 7ft high orcs are standing in the blood pool. They immediately go on the attack. 

The group defeats them after a hard battle and now the next door opens. There is a demon face on an opening at the end of a hallway behind the door. The group camps out in the hallway for a short time to rest up after the battle. After the rest they move towards the mouth of the demon face. Behind they see a ledge to a chasm, on the ledge are skeletons and other dead bodies. They cannot see the bottom of the chasm and there are dark mist filling it, there are stumps of rope going from the bodies to the chasm. At the end of the ledge there is shape of a hand where it seems like you can push your hand into, beside it there are some text they can't read. [[Kaelos]] starts comprehend languages in order to read the text. While casting [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] however just immediately pushes his hand into the form. On the other side of the chasm a giant rift opens and they hear shrieks from the other side of the horrible rift. [[Roland]] recognizes the other side of the rift as the [[Abyssal Plane]]. A giant bird looking creature flies through the rift and towards the group. They defeat it nearly after it almost killing the group. After it dies the portal to the [[Abyssal Plane]] closes.
