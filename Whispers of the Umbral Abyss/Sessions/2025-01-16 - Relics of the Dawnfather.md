### Scene 1 – The Inner Crypt
The group continues deeper into the crypt beneath the Dawnfather chapel in [[Thortil]]. The air grows heavier as they descend, the stone walls marked by age and neglect.

Despite the corruption seen above, traces of the site’s original sanctity remain:
- Faded carvings along the walls
- Symbols of the Dawnfather worn down by time
- A lingering stillness that contrasts with the chaos outside

---

### Scene 2 – The Celestial Tablet
Within one chamber, the group discovers a stone tablet inscribed in celestial.

The text takes the form of a poem dedicated to the Dawnfather. Though its full meaning is not immediately clear, its presence suggests the site once held significant religious importance.

### Scene 3 – The Tomb of Saint Benna
In the next chamber, the group finds a sealed tomb belonging to [[Saint Benna]].

The tomb appears to have been undisturbed until now, its construction more refined than the surrounding ruins.

Upon opening it, they discover:
- A **golden sun**
- A **golden robe**

These relics appear carefully preserved, suggesting importance beyond simple burial items.

The chamber itself feels quieter than the rest of the crypt, as though untouched by the corruption affecting the surrounding area *(uncertain)*
