## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – A City in Decay
The group arrives in [[Stillben]] after crossing the swamp.

They observe:
- Poorly constructed wooden buildings
- High humidity and smell of rot
- Signs of crime and neglect
- Uneven, poorly repaired streets

The city feels unstable and lawless.

---

### Scene 2 – The Sable Note
They are directed to [[The Sable Note]] tavern.

Inside:
- They secure rooms and food
- [[Tassa]] provides information about the city
- They learn about key locations:
  - [[The Dust Vein Curio]]
  - [[The Emberpit forge]]
  - [[The Breakwater Goods and Sundries]]

---

### Scene 3 – Gathering Information
The group explores the city markets.

- [[Kaelos]] gives coins to beggars
- They visit [[The Dust Vein Curio]]
- Meet [[Esree]], an elderly Tiefling merchant
- [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] makes a deal regarding local thieves
- [[Kaelos]] negotiates and sells goods

---

### Scene 4 – The Lead
[[Roland]] casts a spell to locate the [[Suntree Root]].

The magic leads them to the northwestern district:
- Wealthier area
- Large homes and mansions

They arrive at [[Greydawn Hall]].

---

### Scene 5 – The Auction House
A beggar informs [[Kaelos]] that [[Greydawn Hall]]:
- Hosts auctions
- Requires connections and invitations
- Deals in illegal goods

---

### Scene 6 – Preparing the Plan
The group visits [[The Emberpit forge]] briefly before returning to the tavern.

In private, they begin planning how to infiltrate [[Greydawn Hall]] and retrieve the [[Suntree Root]].
