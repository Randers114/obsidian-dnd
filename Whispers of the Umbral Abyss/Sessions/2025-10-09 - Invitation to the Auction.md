## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The Ledger Returned
The group returns to [[The Sable Note]] to deliver the ledger to [[Ves]].

They learn from [[Tassa]] that [[Ves]] has already left for the night.

After briefly reviewing the ledger:
- No direct mentions of [[Ves]] or [[Lorant]]
- Contains transaction logs and guard schedules

They retire for the night.

---

### Scene 2 – Hidden Below
The next day:
- The group shops at [[The Dust Vein Curio]]
  - [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] acquires a cylinder
  - [[Kaelos]] acquires an emberglass eye

Returning to the tavern, they search for [[Ves]].

Through investigation, they uncover an **illusory wall** leading to a hidden basement.

Inside, they find:
- [[Ves]]
- [[Lorant]]

---

### Scene 3 – The Deal Revealed
It becomes clear:
- The ledger was originally meant for [[Lorant]]
- It serves as repayment of a debt involving [[Ves]]

After handing over the ledger:
- [[Lorant]] provides a ticket to the auction
- Confirms the [[Suntree Root]] will be available that night

---

### Scene 4 – Waiting and Wagers
The group waits for evening:
- Engage in gambling
- Each loses 25 gold

---

### Scene 5 – The Auction
The group arrives at [[Greydawn Hall]].

Inside:
- A large hall with a stage
- Four tables arranged before it

Each ticket is marked with symbols:
- [[The Clasp]]
- [[The Myriad]]
- [[Cerberus Assembly]]
- [[Midnight Managerie]]

The auction is about to begin.
