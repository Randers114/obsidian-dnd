# 2026-01-15 - The Hag Wilma and Sesaly's Rescue

## To do

## Recap 


## Summary 
The party navigates a foggy path, battling creatures, and climbs a massive hollow tree. They discover a child's room and eventually confront the hag Wilma, who holds Sesaly in a trance. After a failed negotiation, they defeat Wilma, freeing Sesaly, but lose the opportunity to gain more knowledge about the Aether's creators.

## Story 

The group is attacked by two small creatures with claws. They continue on the path through the fog, while walking  [[Roland]] notices that they are entering a large shadow, he spots in the distance an enormous tree. Approaching the tree they find an opening, and inside the tree is hollowed out, there are winding stairs running up the inside of the tree. They start the climb and has an intuition that the tree is about 100 meters tall. On the winding stairs they find a plateau, where it seems to be a children's room with a bed and a small table where it looks like someone has been coloring. They also see a small shoe on the plateau, when they arrive at the plateau a small tree and a creature with claws appears and attacks them. They defeat the creatures, and continue the climb. 

At the top of the tree that reach another opening out to a giant branch, from this branch the group climbs out to the top of the tree. Here they find an old lady and [[Sesaly]] who looks to be in a trance. The hag [[Wilma]] seems to know about a lot of things and has a lot of insight in how they got there and who they are. They tried to negotiate for [[Sesaly]]'s freedom but she wanted a soul for a soul. [[Kaelos]] offered the [[Aether]] but she could feel that the [[Aether]] was made from animals and not people. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] points his gun at her and a fight ensues, she was strong but she died in the combat, and the group did not find out more about her knowledge of the group that made the [[Aether]]. After the hag died [[Sesaly]] comes out of her trance and collapses in exhaustion.
