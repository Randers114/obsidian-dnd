## Recap 


## Summary 
- The group investigates the exterior of the chapel but finds nothing more, so they decide to return to [[Tallstack Farm]].
- In the guest house, [[Kaelos]] uses comprehend languages to translate a letter found on a cultist, which is addressed to [[Mavrik]].
- The translated letter details that livestock must be prepared and Aether harvested, with the ritual set to occur at [[Silvercut Crossroads]] with [[Zevrin]] during the upcoming Blood Moon’s rise, signed by [[Riven Malakar]].
- Following the tracks leads them to a hill marked by dragging signs, where they encounter a cart and are ambushed by a group of cultists.
- During the attack, one cultist shouts that "[[The Writhing Warden]] won't let you go."
- After defeating the cultists and capturing their leader—whose attire and pin match those from the chapel but differ from earlier cultists—the leader cryptically warns that everyone will pay when [[The Writhing Warden]] rises again, and a letter on him instructs to stage a fake track to halt someone following the trail.
- The group returns to the town, updates [[Joren]], and meets [[Brynja Ironbrow]] at the courthouse, who reveals that during the attack someone transformed into a bat and mentions the area’s history with vampire families, directing them toward the [[Cobalt Soul]].
- The group then sets out for [[Silvercut Crossroads]], where an eerie atmosphere surrounds a monument inscribed with a pledge for justice; their investigation of nearby ruined houses leads to encounters with an undead and a ghost, and ultimately, a descent into a crypt filled with old coffins, animal symbols, and a man in a large room with animals hung on hooks.

## Story 
The group investigates the outside of the chapel and find nothing more. They decide to move back towards [[Tallstack Farm]]. They ask [[Joren]] if they could spend the night and they could and they eat dinner with the family on the farm. After dinner the group goes to the guest house to translate the letter they found on the cultist. 

[[Kaelos]] translates the letter with comprehend languages, and it says: 

**"[[Mavrik]],**

**Our time is near. The livestock must be prepared and the Aether harvested without delay. The blood of these beasts will fuel the next step in our dark ritual, but know this: their sacrifice is only a prelude to greater things. Do not fail.**

**Once you have completed the extraction, bring them to [[Silvercut Crossroads]]. [[Zevrin]] is there to perform the ritual, and we must be ready for the Blood Moon’s rise.** 

**Be swift. Be unseen. Let no outsider stand in your way. The Writhing Warden demands it.**

**[[Riven Malakar]]"**

They spend the night in the guest house. The next day the group moves out from the farm to follow the other unhidden tracks. After following the tracks the group arrives at a hill and they see the dragging marks going to the top of the hill. At the top they see a cart and they get attacked by a group of cultists. One of the cultist yells in the attack that "[[The Writhing Warden]] won't let you go.".

After defeating the cultists and knocking out the leader the group notices that the cultists look different than the ones from the attack on Kymal, they also have a different pin with a another similar symbol as the one from the city. The attire and the pin is however the same as the ones from the chapel. After binding the leader the group interrogates him where he does not give any more information other than saying that everyone will pay when [[The Writhing Warden]] rises again. After killing the leader they found a letter on him stating: 

"**We have received a tip that someone is following the trail. Stage a fake track and make sure they stop.**"

Walking back to the town the group stops quickly at the farm and tells [[Joren]] what has happened. After arriving at the city the group finds [[Brynja Ironbrow]] at the courthouse. She informs that under the attack someone transformed into a bat and that 100's of years ago the area was known to be home to vampire families. She points the group towards the [[Cobalt Soul]] to receive more information. 

The group sets out for [[Silvercut Crossroads]]. the atmosphere in the area around the crossroads seems eerie. There is a monument in the middle of the crossroads and on the monument it has an inscription that states: 

**"The standard-bearers of revolution honor the innocent dead. Vengeance will not restore the lives of the lost, nor will it appease their spirits. We vow you will receive justice, for justice will ensure that no more guiltless blood shall be shed"**

They investigate some the ruins of the houses around the crossroads but find nothing in them. When investigating the houses they are interrupted by an undead that approaches down the road. While investigating some of the other houses they are attacked by a ghost. After dealing with the ghost the group continues investigating the houses outside one of the last houses they see droplets of blood. They see footprints go inside the front door. Inside they find a hatch leading down to a crypt 30 feet underground, they find a lot of coffins that seem very old. The crypt is large and they follow a single set of footprints. They follow them and find a man inside a large room and on the walls were a lot of animals hung from hooks. They also see the symbol they also saw on the farm and on the cultists in the chapel.
