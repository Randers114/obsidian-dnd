## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The Labyrinth Deepens
Continuing through the maze, [[Kaelos]] and [[Roland]] discover a door marked with a bull’s head engraving. It appears non-magical but requires something to unlock it.

They continue searching the labyrinth.

---

### Scene 2 – The Automaton
The group discovers a prison-like area filled with chains and dried blood.

Inside one of the rooms, they find a mechanical figure with a cape. After reactivating it, the construct introduces itself as [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]].

He recalls little beyond being on the run and entering a portal long ago. Freed from his restraints, he joins the group.

---

### Scene 3 – The Maze Fights Back
As they continue:
- They see flickers of themselves *(possible illusions)*
- Hands emerge from walls and restrain [[Roland]]
- They encounter more hostile magical effects

Eventually, they find another gemstone. When [[Kaelos]] lifts it, a mechanism activates elsewhere in the maze.

---

### Scene 4 – The Minotaur Chamber
Returning to the bull-marked door, they find it open.

Inside, a minotaur charges them. After defeating it, they find a massive stone bull head with indentations matching the gemstones.

Placing the gemstones activates the mechanism, causing the bull’s mouth to open and reveal the **third prism**.

---

### Scene 5 – The Convergence
Returning to [[Slaterock Tower]], [[Kaelos]] assembles the three prisms.

They begin to spin and emit a sound before collapsing inward. A scream follows, and a man materializes from the implosion.

He steadies himself and says:
> “Thank god you came.”

*(Identity not yet confirmed)*
