## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – A Tense City
After resting and speaking with [[Eskyld]], the group heads into [[Westruun]] to prepare for their journey to [[Shadebarrow]].

At the gates, they notice the city is under tighter watch than before:
- Guards are checking those entering
- The atmosphere is tense
- Some guards wear the symbol of the new mayor

The group goes directly to [[Gilmore’s Glorious Goods]] to stock up on magical supplies before setting out.

---

### Scene 2 – The Walls of Shadebarrow
Upon reaching [[Shadebarrow]], the group finds a massive stone wall surrounding the fortress.

[[Kaelos]] sends [[Nibs]] over the wall to scout. Through the familiar, he spots five enemies inside.

After getting inside, [[Roland]] recognizes them as members of [[The Ravagers]], a group that worships one of the [[Betrayer Gods]].

The group attacks and kills the ravagers.

---

### Scene 3 – The Fortress Doors
Moving deeper, they come upon a giant door into the fortress itself.

The door is covered in:
- Depictions of torture
- Symbols of the nine [[Betrayer Gods]]

[[Roland]] recognizes the symbols of:
- [[Gruumsh]]
- [[Vecna]]
- [[Asmodeus]]

They force the doors open and enter a vast hall with a ceiling roughly 50 feet high.

At the far end stands another large door marked with the symbol of [[Avandra]], though the symbol has been scratched and partially defaced.

When [[Roland]] touches the faded symbol, the door glows red and opens. He gets the sense that it responded because he is a follower of the gods.

---

### Scene 4 – Blood and Inscription
Beyond the door, the group is attacked by horrific creatures:
- Blobs of blood-like matter
- Covered in mouths

After defeating them, they continue into the next chamber.

There they find:
- A skull-shaped vase
- Withered lilies inside
- Abyssal inscriptions on the table and wall

[[Kaelos]] performs a Comprehend Languages ritual to read them.

The first inscription reads:

> "Cast that which you value into oblivion"

[[Kaelos]] recognizes that “oblivion” here refers to [[Tharistun]].

The second inscription reads:

> "To me touch your hand,  
> To me relinquish hope  
> To you accept death  
> To you invite hollowness"

---

### Scene 5 – The Sacrifice
[[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]], after considering the meaning of the inscription, takes out his beloved hooded lantern.

He drops it into the skull vase.

After a few moments:
- The eye sockets of the vase flash red
- The necklace crumbles to dust
- A hidden wall behind the vase opens

The group gains passage deeper into [[Shadebarrow]].
