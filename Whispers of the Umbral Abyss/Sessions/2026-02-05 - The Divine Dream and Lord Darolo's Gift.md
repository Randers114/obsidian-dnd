# 2026-02-05 - The Divine Dream and Lord Darolo's Gift

## To do
![![Campains/Whispers of the Umbral Abyss/Quest log .md]]

## Recap 


## Summary 
The party returns Sesaly to her mother, briefs Commander Thassian, and explores the city. Roland receives a divine dream about Nefyracsus and the Dawnfather's concerns. C.H.A.R.L.E.S. learns about the special rifle corps. After acquiring provisions, the group meets Lord Darolo, who, after a Zone of Truth spell, provides 4 pounds of Residium as thanks for rescuing Sesaly. C.H.A.R.L.E.S. then challenges Lord Darolo to a shooting contest.

## Story 
The group pick up [[Sesaly]] and start going down the tree stairs. Going down they find [[Sesaly]]'s other shoe. They go back to the city and arrives late in the evening. They go straight to [[Sesaly]]'s mothers [[Aldra]]'s house. [[Aldra]] is very happy that they brought back her daughter, she puts her to bed and comes back to thank the group for bringing her back. 

They leave [[Aldra]]'s house, and make their way towards the city watch commander [[Thassian]] to inform him about that they found the girl. He informs them that he might be able to get them a meeting with the leadership of the city the next day. Finding the girl will help them to be in better standing when asking the city leadership for Residium. 

They leave the office and go back to their inn [[The Sun's Delight]] where they get some food. After the meal [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] goes for a walk around the city to find a store that has some guns. They saw earlier when walking around the city that some of the guards in the city had firearms. He found a store that the group had seen before in [[Westruun]], namely [[Gilmore’s Glorious Goods]], he could not find any firearms in there. [[Roland]] goes out into the city also to find a temple to [[The Dawnfather]] in order to pray. [[The Dawnfather]] is the main religion in the city and therefore the temple is quite large and there is a lot of foot traffic. He kneels in the temple and prays to his god. 

[[Kaelos]] goes out into the city as well to find incense in order to summon his familiar again [[Nibs]]. But finds that all the stores are closed this time of night, he goes back to the inn in order to go to sleep and [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] and [[Roland]] joins him. During the night [[Roland]] has a dream where [[The Dawnfather]] visits him. He asks for guidance against their enemies [[The Writhing Warden]], he mention [[Nefyracsus]] which is one of the elder demon lords from the abyss. He lives by stealing the life force and essence of other demons in the abyss. Many years ago he was imprisoned in a [[Affyrimental]] where he should be imprisoned for eternity. [[The Dawnfather]] shares his concerns that he might be freed from his prison because of the movements of the different factions. He says that he believes that [[Roland]] will make the right call when the time arrives, and he has not made it possible for him to find his shield if he did not believe in them. 

The next day [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] goes out into the city again and stops the first guards he sees with a gun and asks about it. The guard says that he is a member of the special rifle core, they train under the lord of [[Whitestone]], who is also the inventor of guns. Most of the core is stations at the castle in the city. The special rifle core is a small group in the city and it is prestigious to be a member of, there are only 30 members. 

[[Roland]] and [[Kaelos]] goes towards [[Gilmore’s Glorious Goods]] in order to find provisions. [[Kaelos]] buys incense from the shop owner, and [[Roland]] asks about different magic items to buy. He shows them a Bag of Holding and a belt that makes it so that everything they throw returns to the person who threw the item. Both items were however too expensive for the group, the belt for 450 gold and the bag for 750 gold. After the shop the group goes to meet [[Thassian]] by the Suntree. He says the they can follow him immediately and meet the lord of the city. But [[Roland]] says he quickly has to stop by [[Sesaly]]'s place and see if she was in the need of healing after she was saved yesterday. Hen then rejoins the group and they follow [[Thassian]] towards the city lord, they arrive at a castle and gets lead inside. 

Inside they see multiple pictures of a family with children one of them features a #tiefling also. They are lead into a room that looks like a giant study, where [[Lord Darolo]] is waiting for them. He seems interested in [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] robotics and guns as he is the inventor of guns in this realms. [[Roland]] explains to him that they had found some Suntree root that they had delivered back to [[Whitestone]] after taken a piece of it. [[Roland]] casts a zone of truth on the group in order to get the trust of [[Lord Darolo]].  [[Lord Darolo]] proceeds to immediately ask if they were sent here by [[Eskyld]] as he has heard the gathering of the ingredients before. 

After some discussion [[Lord Darolo]] goes to his desk and pulls out 4 pounds of Residium, he throws it to [[Roland]] and says that it is in thanks for helping the city when finding [[Sesaly]]. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] challenges [[Lord Darolo]] to a shooting contest.
