## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – Through the Mist
The group follows the newly revealed path through the fog.

Along the way, they are attacked by two small clawed creatures. After defeating them, they continue forward.

As they travel deeper, [[Roland]] notices that they are passing into a massive shadow. In the distance, the group sees an enormous tree.

---

### Scene 2 – The Hollow Tree
Approaching the tree, they find an opening leading inside.

The tree is hollowed out, with winding stairs spiraling upward through the interior. The group estimates the tree to be around 100 meters tall.

Partway up, they come across a plateau containing what appears to be a child’s room:
- A bed
- A small table
- Evidence of coloring or drawing
- A small shoe

As they enter the plateau, a small tree-creature and another clawed creature attack. The group defeats them and continues climbing.

---

### Scene 3 – At the Crown
At the top of the tree, the group emerges onto a giant branch and climbs to the crown.

There they find:
- [[Sesaly]], trapped in a trance-like state
- An old hag named [[Wilma]]

[[Wilma]] appears to know much about the group, how they arrived, and who they are.

The group attempts to bargain for [[Sesaly]]’s freedom, but [[Wilma]] demands:
> a soul for a soul

[[Kaelos]] offers [[Aether]], but [[Wilma]] rejects it after sensing that it was made from animals rather than people.

---

### Scene 4 – The Hag Falls
When negotiations fail, [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] raises his gun and combat begins.

[[Wilma]] proves dangerous, but the group eventually kills her.

With the hag dead, [[Sesaly]] comes out of her trance and collapses from exhaustion.

Though the girl is saved, the group is left without answers about how much [[Wilma]] knew—or what she might have revealed if she had lived.
