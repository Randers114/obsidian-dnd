## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – Percival’s Workshop
After the shooting contest, which [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] loses, [[Percival]] still offers to temper his firearm.

He leads the group into a workshop in the basement of the castle and gives [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] a choice:
- greater distance
- greater power

[[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] chooses improved range.

However, the tempering does not go as intended. Although the gun is modified for greater range, a fault is introduced into the weapon, making it less effective overall.

---

### Scene 2 – Arrival in Turst Field
The group departs [[Whitestone]] and travels toward [[Westruun]], planning to stop in [[Turst Field]] to investigate the stolen Residium shipment.

In the city, they notice that unlike many other places they have visited, a number of #gnolls are openly incorporated into local life.

They know from [[Thassian]] that:
- the shipment was stolen by [[The Myriad]]
- it is being held in a hideout in the city
- a contact named [[Gwen]] may be able to help
- they have blueprints of the hideout

---

### Scene 3 – Planning the Infiltration
[[Roland]] starts by asking about [[Gwen]] at the inn, but the owner does not recognize the name immediately and says he will ask his wife.

The group retreats to their room to examine the blueprint and discuss their options.

[[Kaelos]] asks about access to the cistern and learns:
- the official entrance at the bank is sealed
- there may be a way down east of the city

The group heads east and finds a steep descent leading to an opening into the cistern below.

---

### Scene 4 – Beneath the City
Moving through the cistern, the group hears footsteps echoing through the tunnels. [[Roland]] tries to follow them, but the maze-like acoustics make it difficult.

Eventually, they find the entrance to the hideout:
- a smooth marble door
- no handles
- seemingly sealed by magic or mechanism

Before they can solve it alone, they are interrupted by [[Gwen]].

---

### Scene 5 – The Door Opens
[[Gwen]] explains that she came down by rope and describes herself as an independent third party who does not want the Residium falling into the wrong hands.

She knows the passphrase and speaks it aloud.

The marble door slides open.

The group enters the hideout with [[Gwen]], and combat with bandits begins.
