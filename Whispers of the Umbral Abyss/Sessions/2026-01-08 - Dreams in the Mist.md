## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – Return to the Tower
After returning the wagon in [[Westruun]], the group heads back to [[Slaterock Tower]] and reunites with [[Eskyld]].

They hand over roughly 40% of the [[Suntree Root]], which [[Eskyld]] says should be enough for about two attempts at creating the portal.

The group rests, with [[Kaelos]] briefly returning to [[Westruun]] to buy two healing potions. They also part ways with [[Hotesh]] before continuing on.

---

### Scene 2 – Arrival in Whitestone
[[Eskyld]] teleports the group to [[Whitestone]].

On arrival, they notice:
- A castle to the north
- A white tower to the east

They secure rooms at [[The Sun's Delight]] and then head toward the [[Suntree]] to report what they know about the root.

There they meet the watch commander, who turns out to be [[Thassian]], an old army friend of [[Roland]].

[[Thassian]] explains that he may be able to arrange an audience with someone who can approve the group’s request to buy Residium, but helping the city first would improve their chances.

He offers two leads:
- A missing shipment of Residium, stolen by [[The Myriad]] and now located in [[Turst Field]]
- A missing girl named [[Sesaly]]

He also mentions a spy named [[Gwen]] who can help if they pursue the shipment.

---

### Scene 3 – Sesaly’s Home
The group chooses to investigate the missing girl first and is led to the home of her mother, [[Aldra]].

[[Aldra]] explains:
- [[Sesaly]] disappeared two nights ago
- She had recently begun speaking about seeing her dead father, [[Garan]], in dreams
- [[Aldra]] herself has also had nightmares about her late husband for the past two weeks
- On the morning of the disappearance, [[Sesaly]]’s window was open and tracks led away from it

Searching the room, [[Roland]] finds:
- [[Sesaly]]’s journal
- A drawing of an old woman with her father in the woods
- A doll smelling of withered petals
- Loose withered flower petals

[[Aldra]] says the doll was acquired only a few weeks ago, after [[Sesaly]] had been out with friends.

---

### Scene 4 – Julia’s Story
The group visits [[Julia]], one of [[Sesaly]]’s friends, who is playing in the front garden.

She says:
- She and [[Sesaly]] used to play in the woods
- She has also dreamed of an old woman standing in fog
- [[Sesaly]] had started talking to herself

She leads the group to the place where they used to play.

---

### Scene 5 – The Clearing
The group finds an unnaturally quiet clearing in the woods.

After escorting [[Julia]] back home, they return to investigate.

In the center of the clearing, [[Kaelos]] casts detect magic and senses:
- Illusion magic
- Enchantment magic
- Faint silver threads extending upward

After a moment, a path opens through the mist.

The group chooses to follow it deeper into the woods.
