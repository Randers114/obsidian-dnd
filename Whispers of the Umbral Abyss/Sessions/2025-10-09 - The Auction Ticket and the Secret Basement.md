## Story 

After stealing the ledger they return to the tavern to give the ledger to [[Ves]]. After asking [[Tassa]] about [[Ves]] they are informed that she has gone home that day. It is the middle of the night at this point. After reading a bit in the ledger they find no specific information about [[Ves]] or [[Lorant]] but there are some information about transactions and guard schedules. They return to the room to sleep for the night. 

The group goes back to [[The Dust Vein Curio]] and buys a cylinder for [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] and an emberglass eye for [[Kaelos]]. They return to the inn in order to find [[Ves]]. She is not at the tavern says [[Tassa]], but after some investigation the group finds an illusory wall that leads to a basement. In the basement they find [[Ves]] and [[Lorant]] sitting together at a table. [[Kaelos]] walks over and it becomes apparent that the ledger was originally for [[Lorant]] to pay a debt she has to him. Handing over the ledger, he produces a ticket to the auction and informs that the [[Suntree Root]] is available for auction the same night and that they should know where it takes place. [[Kaelos]] takes the ticket and walks over to the others. They have taken up gambling at another shady table at the inn. They each lost 25 gold in the game. They return upstairs. 

On the ticket they find symbols for: [[The Clasp]], [[The Myriad]], [[Serverus Assembly]] and [[Midnight Managerie]].  The group awaits the evening. They go over to the auction at [[Greydawn Hall]]. Arriving at the auction they give the ticket and is lead into a large hall where there are 4 tables with people sitting around in front of a large stage.
