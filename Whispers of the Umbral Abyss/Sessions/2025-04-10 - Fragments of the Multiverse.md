## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The Realmseer Revealed
The man released from the prisms identifies himself as [[Eskyld]], the Realmseer.

He explains that his experiment went wrong, causing him to be split apart at a molecular level.

[[Roland]] heals him, helping him recover.

[[Eskyld]] reveals:
- His goal was to open a portal to [[Siegel]], a central hub of the multiverse
- His past with [[Arcana Pansophical]], where he was a Maker
- He left the organization after being ridiculed

He also reveals:
- [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] resembles a warforged from [[Eboron]]
- The existence of the multiverse

---

### Scene 2 – The Missing Components
[[Eskyld]] explains he needs three components to complete his experiment:

- **Residuum (10 pounds)** → [[Whitestone]]
- **Root of the Suntree** → [[Stillben]]
- **Divine Tear** → [[Shadebarrow]]

He notes:
- Residuum is controlled
- Suntree roots are in the hands of thieves
- Divine tear is difficult to locate

This creates tension with [[Roland]], as the components relate to [[The Dawnfather]].

---

### Scene 3 – Preparation
The group returns to [[Westruun]] and purchases:
- Healing potions
- Immovable rod

They then return to [[Eskyld]].

---

### Scene 4 – Journey to Sephrah
[[Eskyld]] teleports the group to [[Sephrah]].

They arrive at a monastery and learn:
- [[Stillben]] is nearby (about a day’s travel)
- Genasi have been kidnapped
- A criminal organization may be responsible
- The kidnappings lead further into the mountains

---

### Scene 5 – A New Path
The group prepares to head toward [[Stillben]], now facing:
- Eskyld’s mission
- Regional instability
- A new set of unknown enemies
