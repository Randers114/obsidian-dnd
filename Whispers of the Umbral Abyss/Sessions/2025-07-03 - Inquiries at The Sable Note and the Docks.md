## Summary 
- The party inquired at The Sable Note tavern about the Greydawn Hall auction.
- Tassa suggested talking to Lorant, the tavern owner, and a singer who might have information.
- The singer agreed to help the party get into the auction if they retrieved a ledger from the customs captain at the docks.
- Roland bribed a guard to gain access to the restricted water watch warehouse, where Kaelos sent his familiar inside.
- The group subsequently entered the forest to investigate reports of attacks on townspeople.



## Story 

In the morning in [[The Sable Note]] tavern they go down into the common area and talk to [[Tassa]] about the [[Greydawn Hall]] auction and she mentions that [[Lorant]] who is the owner of the tavern has some connections to it. He will be in the tavern later that day. She also mentions that [[Singer]] who was the singer from the day before, might also have some information about it. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] goes over and talks to her. She mentions that she can help the group get into the auction if they could do them a small favor. She want the group to find a ledger from the customs captain at the docs. 

The group goes to the docs but down by the water where the water watch resides is off limits for the public. [[Roland]] bribes the guard for access to the water watch warehouse and [[Kaelos]] familiar follows inside while invisible. They leave him inside for now. After this they enter the forest in order to find the people attacking the people from town in order to steal their goods.
