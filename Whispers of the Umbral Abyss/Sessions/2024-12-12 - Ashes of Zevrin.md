## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The End of Zevrin
In the depths beneath [[Silvercut Crossroads]], the group confronts the man in the ritual chamber. He reveals himself as [[Zevrin]] and threatens the group before drinking from a canister.

Despite this, the group defeats him. Before his death, he references the [[Sect of The Writhing Warden]].

Searching his body, they find:
- An amulet bearing the eye symbol seen previously
- Several canisters containing a substance tied to Aether *(exact nature unknown)*

---

### Scene 2 – A Patron’s Demand
While resting, [[Kaelos]] is awakened by an ice fiend appearing in the fireplace. She reveals herself as his patron and gives him a task: retrieve **concentrated Aether**.

The encounter is private, and the rest of the group only learns about it the next day.

---

### Scene 3 – The Road to Westruun
The group begins traveling toward [[Westruun]] and the [[Cobalt Soul]].

Along the way:
- They meet a traveler who shares news of unrest in the city
- A new leader has been appointed: [[Lady Cerafis Thaldrin]]
- The appointment is notable as she is the first woman to hold the position

During the journey, [[Kaelos]] sends his familiar ahead to scout. He later reveals that his patron provided information: the kidnapped people are being held in [[Thortil]].

---

### Scene 4 – Arrival in Westruun
The group arrives in [[Westruun]] and makes their way to the [[Cobalt Soul]].

Before settling in, they:
- Purchase travel supplies
- Explore the city

They visit [[Gilmore’s Glorious Goods]], where they meet Daisy, a shop assistant. Gilmore himself is not present.
