# 2026-01-08 - Whitestone, Sesaly, and the Misty Path

## To do

## Recap 


## Summary 
The party delivers Suntree Root to Eskyld at Slaterock Tower, then travels to Whitestone. There, they meet Commander Thassian, who tasks them with finding a kidnapped girl named Sesaly and a missing Residium shipment stolen by the Myriad in Turst Field. Investigating Sesaly's disappearance, they learn about her dreams and a mysterious doll, leading them to a magical clearing in the woods where a path through the mist appears.

## Story 

After handing back the wagon in [[Westruun]] the group heads to [[Slaterock Tower]] to meet up with [[Eskyld]]. They hand about 40% of the Root of the Suntree to [[Eskyld]], this will give about 2 attempts to create the portal. [[Kaelos]] goes into [[Westruun]] and buys 2 healing potions for the group and then they rest for the rest of the day. They bid farewell to [[Hotesh]] and go to [[Eskyld]] who teleports the group to [[Whitestone]]. After they arrive they can see a castle to the north and a white tower to the east. They find a tavern to get a room called [[The Sun's Delight]], after this they go towards the Suntree. 

[[Roland]] talks to one of the guards to find the watch commander so they can inform about the Suntree root. Walking through the city to find the commander they see a very popular bakery, the name is [[Slayers Cake Bakery]]. After arriving at the Suntree they see that there is no fence around the tree, but there is a lot of guards, one of them have a golden cape and he is the commander. As they approach the commander [[Roland]] recognizes him as a friend from the army [[Thassian]]. They follow [[Thassian]] to his office to talk, and tell him about the Suntree root they found. He says that he can give the groups an audience with someone who can decide if they can buy Residium. He also says that they can improve their chances by helping them. A girl has been kidnapped from the city and they need help finding her, he also says that they need help finding a missing shipment of Residium. The missing shipment of Residium has been intercepted by the [[Myriad]] and is in the city of [[Turst Field]]. The missing girl is a human girl named [[Sesaly]] she went missing a few days ago, her mother does not know where she has gone. She disappeared in the night without any signs of a break in in the house. In regards to the missing shipment he also tells that they have found a blueprint of the building where the shipment is being held, at the [[Myriad]] hideout, the shipment was 5 pounds. He also says that he has a spy in the area and the group can contact her if the go after the shipment, her name is [[Gwen]]. 

After the meeting they follow a guard towards the mother of [[Sesaly]]'s house. The mothers name is [[Aldra]]. She says that 2 nights ago was when she disappeared, she went early to bed, after saying that she would see her dad in her dreams. Her dad died years ago. In the morning she found that her window was open which is usually locked, she found her tracks leading away from the window. Her husbands name was [[Garan]] and she have also had nightmares about her husband in the last two weeks which was when her daughters dreams started. [[Roland]] searches [[Sesaly]]'s room and find her journal, a page with a drawing an old woman with her dad in the woods, a doll that smells of withered petals, and some withered flower petals. She says that she got the dolls a few weeks ago, after being out with her friends. [[Aldra]] says that they can talk with her friends about where they got the doll and [[Julia]] lives right down the street. 

They found [[Julia]] playing in the front garden, her parents are not home. They convince her to show where they used to play in the woods. [[Julia]] also says that she has had dreams about an old woman in the woods in a fog. She also says that [[Sesaly]] started talking to herself. After finding the place they used to play, they find an unnaturally silent clearing, they lead [[Julia]] back to her home and return to the clearing. In the middle of the clearing [[Kaelos]] casts detect magic. [[Kaelos]] gets a hint of illusion and enchantment magic, after some time he also gets a hint of a silver threads going upwards. A path opens through the mist and the group decides to follow the path out of the clearing into the woods.
