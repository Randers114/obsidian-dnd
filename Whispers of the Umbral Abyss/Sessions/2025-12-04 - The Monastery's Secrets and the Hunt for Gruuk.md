# To do

## Recap 


## Summary 
* Following the defeat of the air elemental and the closing of the portal, the party discovers a skeleton wearing an amulet with a symbol from the monastery of [[Sephrah]] and magical boots.
* They return to the palace in [[Sephrah]] to meet with [[Hotesh]], receiving payment for their task.
* The party learns about the training of blade dancers and druids in the palace, led by [[Leada]] and [[Maeve]] respectively.
* Seeking information on the [[Sect of The Writhing Warden]], they are advised to consult [[Jores Thall]], the headmaster of the monastery.
* They meet [[Jores Thall]], who recognizes the amulet as belonging to his former master and takes possession of it.
* [[Jores Thall]] identifies the staff taken from [[Severin]] as belonging to a former, aggressive student of the monastery.
* He reveals that another former student, [[Gruuk Stonejaw]], a Ganashi, was a bad influence on [[Severin]] and likely knows more about the cult.
* The party decides to leave [[Sephrah]] and prepares to travel to [[Slaterock Tower]] to meet with [[Eskyld]] with the [[Suntree Root]] in their possession.

## Story 

After the portal was closed and the air elemental was defeated the group takes a small break and heal up. Behind the portal they find a ruin with a skeleton with an amulet around its neck. On the amulet is a symbol of the monk order from the monastery [[Sephrah]] they also find some magical boots under the skeleton which they take back with them. 

After they arrive back in [[Sephrah]] they go with [[Maeve]] back to the palace and find [[Hotesh]]. They also decide to spend the night there. In the palace they are training 16 children to be either blade dancers or druids. [[Maeve]] is the trainer for the druids and [[Leada]] is the trainer for the blade dancers. They find a room and go to sleep, after they receive the payment for the job of closing the portal. The groups also asks [[Maeve]] about the [[Sect of The Writhing Warden]], and she says that they should talk to [[Jores Thall]] in the monastery, he is the headmaster there, he belongs to [[Order of the Tranquil Mind]]. They also ask about [[The Umbral Abyss]] and vampires, she informs them that she does not know anything about them. 

The group goes to the monastery,, when they arrive at the monastery, they see there is a bit more lively there than last time which was right after the kidnapping. They find [[Jores Thall]] in his office, he is an old man. They tell [[Jores Thall]] about the ruin in the mountains and show him the amulet they found, he seemed to recognize the amulet. He says that the amulet belonged to his old master, the previous headmaster of the monastery. They give [[Jores Thall]] the amulet and he keeps it. After [[Roland]] walks into the room [[Jores Thall]] starts staring at his staff and asks where he got it, they inform him that they got it from [[Severin]] after trying to kill them and they killed him. He informs that [[Severin]] was a student at the monastery earlier, and that [[Severin]] were always hotheaded and aggressive in his training while he as at the monastery. He left the monastery 5 years ago. He also says that there was another student named [[Gruuk Stonejaw]] who was a Ganashi and a bad influence on [[Severin]]. [[Gruuk Stonejaw]] might know more about the cult of [[The Writhing Warden]]. 

The group goes back into the city and rents a wagon in order to travel back to [[Slaterock Tower]] and move out from the city. During the travels they contact [[Eskyld]] wizard and tell him that they should arrive within 3 days and and that they have the [[Suntree Root]].
