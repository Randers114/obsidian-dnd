## Recap 


## Summary 
- The party traveled through the swamp towards [[Sephrah]] while carrying the secured [[Suntree Root]].
- [[Roland]] confronted a group of trackers following them; the resulting combat led to the party's victory.
- Looting the defeated trackers revealed a [[Loxon]] badge and a threatening letter signed "D" demanding the [[Suntree Root]].
- Upon returning to [[Sephrah]], the party met with [[Maeve]] to address the issue with the elementals near the portal.
- Guided by [[Maeve]], the party successfully closed the rogue portal to the [[Plane of Air]] while defeating the guarding elemental.

## Story 

After defeating the people coming after [[Hotesh]] and getting the [[Suntree Root]] from them, the group decides to go out in the swamp back towards [[Sephrah]]. While walking the path [[Roland]] hears something in the swamp following the group, it is a rattling in the vegetation. After setting up camp [[Roland]] walks back towards the people following and calls out to them. They need the [[Suntree Root]] and are prepared to fight for it. [[Roland]] tries to convince them that it would be a bad idea to try and take it by force, [[Roland]] starts to walk back to the group but they run after him and start a fight.  

After defeating the group they find some gold on them and find a badge with a symbol on it that is of the [[Loxon]]. They also find a letter with a description of a task they had full of threats that they should find the [[Suntree Root]] or else, it is signed "D". After a resting the night, the group moves towards [[Sephrah]] again. Coming back to [[Sephrah]] the group decides to seek out [[Maeve]] in order to see if they still need help with the problem with the elementals near the portal to the [[Plane of Air]]. 

After meeting [[Maeve]] she says they need help to close a rouge portal to the [[Plane of Air]], she will close the portal while the group should distract the large elemental holding it open. She says that they should leave for the portal as soon as possible. The groups decides to leave with her immediately. She managed to close the portal while the group took care of the large elemental.
