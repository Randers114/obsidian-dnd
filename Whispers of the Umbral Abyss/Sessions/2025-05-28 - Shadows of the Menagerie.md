## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – A Familiar Mark
While in [[Sephrah]], the group overhears a conversation led by [[Leada]], the captain of the city guard, discussing recent kidnappings of Genasi in the surrounding region.

During the conversation, [[Kaelos]] notices an insignia carried by one of the individuals involved in the discussion. He recognizes it as belonging to the [[Midnight Managerie]]—the same organization that once captured and enslaved him.

The realization connects the current kidnappings directly to his past

[[Leada]] mentions that she can be reached through the city guard at the castle if more information is needed.

The group questions a monk nearby about where the kidnapped individuals were taken and where the insignia was found, giving them a direction to follow into the mountains.

---

### Scene 2 – Into the Mountains
Following the trail of the kidnappers, the group ascends into the mountains.

They discover a guarded encampment, with Orcs acting as sentries. The presence of the [[Midnight Managerie]] insignia confirms the connection.

They eliminate the outer guards and free a captured lizardfolk.

---

### Scene 3 – The Cave System
The group enters a cave leading deeper into the mountain.

Inside, they find:
- Multiple captives
- Signs of prolonged imprisonment
- Active enforcers guarding the operation

They systematically free the captives and engage the remaining enemies.

---

### Scene 4 – Breaking the Chains
After defeating the enforcers, the group clears the camp.

The structure of the operation mirrors what [[Kaelos]] experienced in the past:
- Captured individuals
- Forced labor or containment
- Organized criminal control

The group eliminates the threat and frees all remaining captives.

---

### Scene 5 – Echoes of the Past
With the camp destroyed, the group is left with the weight of what this means for [[Kaelos]].

This was not just another mission—it was a direct confrontation with the kind of group that once held him captive.
