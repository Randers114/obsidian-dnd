## Story 
The [[Wayfarers Accord]] starts looking into solve the next puzzle. After solving the senses puzzle, the next doors open revealing a new puzzle. After solving the last a gold dome opens up and shows a floating prism reflecting the light. [[Roland]] goes over and takes the prism. They then decide to go back to the office in [[Slaterock Tower]]. The first portal they came from did not close after coming back to the tower, they decide to go into the third portal with the darkness. Inside they arrive on a big wall, it seems to be inside a cube with gravity shifted onto each side. They can see the sky through windows in the walls and the sky is littered with stars and constellations. After coming inside two incorporeal things attack them. [[Roland]] calls out that it is a Wraith and a Specter. They defeat the undead, and looking up to the top of the cube they seem to be mirrored. So they see themselves on the ceiling, [[Kaelos]] move to one of the sides that has another portal than the one they entered from. When he jumped to climb for it gravity shifted to the side and he could now walk on the wall. 

The group jumped through the portal, on the other side they find a winding staircase of bones protruding form the wall. They start walking down and they see faces protruding from the wall along with the bone steps, looking closer they see that it is porcelain masks showing different facial expressions. [[Roland]] decides to cast some healing magic on himself and as he does so two gargoyle statues come to life in the corners and starts flying towards them. During the fight [[Thaldrin]]tries to help [[Roland]] and jumps between the bone steps, he loses balance and falls to his death. After that [[Kaelos]] and [[Roland]] goes to the bottom and goes through the portal. They get another prism and go back outside the portal to rest in the office. 

They decide to keep going even though [[Thaldrin]] died in the other portal. Emerging through the third portal, they find themselves in a humid hot crypt with a putrid smell. There are a few doors and they go through one of them to find a long corridor which seems to be a maze of some sort. Coming to a dead end they see for a short second a copy of themselves standing there. They keep going through the maze and find a giant emerald on a pedestal. Going further they find another dead end with a shimmering wall, [[Kaelos]] gets the impression that it is covered in illusion magic, they throw some things at it but nothing happens. They decide to keep going and find a large amber lump on another pedestal. In the next room they find a giant ruby. They immerge in a new dead end here [[Roland]] says that it feels like the wall is awaiting them and [[Kaelos]] gets the impression of transmutation magic. They go up to the wall and is attacked by a mimic. They defeat the mimic wall.
