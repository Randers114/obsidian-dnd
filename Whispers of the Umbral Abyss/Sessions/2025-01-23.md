## Story 

After sleeping in an abandoned house in the ruined city the group sets out to find where the cultists are located. They find the headquarters and get attacked by a large group of cultists. After taking care of the cultists the group finds a lot of hostages that they free. They tell them that there were a lot more hostages and that they have been taken to the south. They decide to take the hostages to safety and run to the north of the city where they run into a patrol. They defeat the patrol and guide the hostages about 20 min north of the city where they tell them to go back to [[Kymal]] and tlell [[Brynja Ironbrow]] what happened.
