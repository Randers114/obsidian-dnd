## 📖 Summary
The group attends the auction at [[Greydawn Hall]], where rare and illicit goods are sold. While they fail to secure the [[Suntree Root]], they instead free a captured Loxodon named [[Hotesh]]. After learning more about the forces behind the trade, they devise a plan to ambush the buyer of the root.

## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The Auction Begins
The auction opens with several items:
- Spices (73 gold)
- Obsidian dagger (317 gold)
- Pre-calamity scrolls (795 gold)

[[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] notices sounds of chiseling from the back rooms.

---

### Scene 2 – A Dark Turn
[[Roland]] overhears a priestly woman speaking about acquiring slaves.

The next item:
- A Loxodon, presented as an exotic creature from the [[Midnight Managerie]]

[[Roland]] enters a bidding war and wins the Loxodon for 375 gold.

---

### Scene 3 – The Head Item
Further items are sold, including:
- Water-walking ring
- Ancient map

The final major item:
- [[Suntree Root]]

It is purchased for 1000 gold by the same woman:
👉 [[Sister Grieva]]

---

### Scene 4 – A New Ally
The group speaks with the auction speaker:
- Learns the slave came from [[Midnight Managerie]]
- Their main base is in [[Emon]]

They free the Loxodon:
- His name is [[Hotesh]]
- Offer him a place with them at [[Slaterock Tower]]

---

### Scene 5 – The Plan
The group devises a plan:
- Use [[Hotesh]] as bait
- Ambush [[Sister Grieva]] outside the city
- Retrieve the [[Suntree Root]]

They prepare and initiate the ambush.
