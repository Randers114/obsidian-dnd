## 📖 Summary
Returning to [[Thortil]], the group discovers that the city has fallen eerily silent and that bodies of fallen cultists have been removed. Following the trail, they uncover a hidden estate where Aether is being actively produced. After defeating the responsible cultist, they uncover a deeper connection between the cult and a vampire organization known as [[The Umbral Abyss]]. The session culminates in [[Kaelos]] delivering Aether to his patron, [[Malvoria]], revealing her identity and infernal origin.

## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The Silent Return
After escorting the rescued captives to safety, the group makes camp outside [[Thortil]]. The following morning, they return to the city to continue their efforts.

The city feels different—quieter. The activity and tension that once filled the streets have faded into an unnatural stillness.

---

### Scene 2 – The Vanishing Dead
Returning to the mansion where they previously fought cultists, the group notices that all the corpses have been removed.

Drag marks lead away from the site, and the group follows them through the city to another estate that appears barricaded.

---

### Scene 3 – The Hidden Laboratory
Inside the estate, the group discovers signs of forced movement and concealment. Behind a statue, they uncover a hidden passage leading down into a basement.

Below, the evidence becomes clear: bodies have been dragged here, and the space is being used for some form of experimentation.

---

### Scene 4 – The Aether Architect
In one of the rooms, the group encounters a woman who claims the operation as her own—an experiment to create [[Aether]].

A battle ensues, and after defeating her and her allies, the group captures and interrogates her.

She reveals:
- She operates under orders from [[Theran]]
- She is part of [[The Umbral Abyss]], an organization of vampires

---

### Scene 5 – A Patron Revealed
After the encounter, [[Kaelos]] calls upon his patron and delivers the recovered [[Aether]].

The patron reveals herself as [[Malvoria]], a being tied to [[Cania]], the ninth layer of hell.

This confirms the personal stakes of the Aether beyond the cult’s use.
