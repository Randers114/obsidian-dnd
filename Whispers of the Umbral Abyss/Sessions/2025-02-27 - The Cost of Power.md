## 📖 Summary
The group eliminates the captured vampire and leaves [[Thortil]], returning to [[Kymal]] to regroup. After consulting [[Brynja Ironbrow]], they seek knowledge from the [[Cobalt Soul]] in [[Westruun]]. Along the journey, tensions and personal histories surface. At the Cobalt Soul, the group learns the true danger of Aether: it grants power at the cost of life and, in sufficient quantities, can be used to summon or free demon lords.

## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The Interrogation
The group questions the captured vampire in the basement, attempting to learn more about the organizations behind the recent events. However, she provides little useful information, either due to ignorance or unwillingness.

The group ultimately decides to kill her before moving on.

---

### Scene 2 – Leaving Thortil
Before departing, the group searches the mansion and recovers two spell scrolls.

They leave the ruined city of [[Thortil]] behind and return to [[Kymal]] to report their findings.

At the guard station, they speak with [[Brynja Ironbrow]], who advises them to consult the [[Cobalt Soul]] regarding the Aether canisters and suggests seeking knowledge from the Air Ashari in [[Sephrah]].

---

### Scene 3 – The Road to Westruun
The group prepares and sets out toward [[Westruun]].

During the journey, [[Thaldrin]] shares part of his past—revealing that he once had his shoes taken while being held captive, and has struggled to find footwear that does not cause him pain since.

Later, [[Roland]] detects movement off the path, and the group is ambushed by ravagers. They defeat the attackers and continue onward.

---

### Scene 4 – Knowledge of Aether
At the [[Cobalt Soul]], the group meets [[Lorvein Drenner]] and shares what they have learned.

She provides crucial information:
- Vampires are known to signal activity in the north, around [[Whitestone]]
- Aether:
  - Grants increased power when consumed
  - Drains the user’s life force
  - In sufficient quantities, can be used to **free or summon demon lords**

She performs a ritual on the Aether canister but gains only limited additional insight.

---

### Scene 5 – A Dangerous Truth
With this knowledge, the group begins to understand the true scale of the threat.

What began as local attacks and disappearances may now be part of a larger effort to bring a **demon lord into the world**.
