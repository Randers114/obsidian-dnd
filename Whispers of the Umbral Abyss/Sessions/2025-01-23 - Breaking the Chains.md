## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – A Night in Ruin
The group takes shelter in an abandoned house within the ruined city of [[Thortil]]. The structure offers little comfort, but provides enough cover to rest before continuing their search.

The silence of the city is broken only by distant movement and the lingering sense that the group is not alone.

---

### Scene 2 – The Cult Stronghold
Setting out the next day, the group searches for signs of organized activity and eventually locates what appears to be a central gathering point for the cultists.

They are quickly engaged by a large group of enemies. After a difficult fight, the group manages to defeat them and secure the area.

---

### Scene 3 – The Freed
Inside, they discover a number of captives held by the cult. The group frees them and gathers what information they can.

The survivors reveal:
- Many more captives had been held previously
- These individuals were recently moved south
- The movement appeared organized and deliberate

---

### Scene 4 – Escape and Escort
The group decides to prioritize the safety of those they rescued.

While moving north out of the city, they encounter a patrol of cultists. After defeating them, they continue onward until they are far enough from [[Thortil]] to be relatively safe.

Roughly twenty minutes north of the city, the group instructs the survivors to make their way back to [[Kymal]] and report to [[Brynja Ironbrow]].

---

### Scene 5 – The Trail Continues
With the rescued civilians sent to safety, the group is left with a new direction: south.

The realization sets in that the threat is not contained to [[Thortil]]—and that the ritual may still be progressing elsewhere.
