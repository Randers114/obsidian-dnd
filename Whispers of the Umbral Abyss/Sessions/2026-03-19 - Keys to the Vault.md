## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The Corner Office
After pushing deeper into the hideout, the group reaches a corner room where they find the leader of the operation, guarded by two shadow mastiffs.

The group attacks and defeats them.

Searching her body, they find:
- Platinum
- A note containing:
  - 1. Kitchen
  - 2. Meeting room
  - 3. Suite

They also search her office and uncover numerous orders related to criminal activity.

[[Gwen]] reveals that she is helping the group on behalf of her family, and specifically because of her father. She also recognizes the crest on the Residium order that [[Roland]] had found, identifying it as the symbol of the [[The Myriad]] department in [[Emon]].

---

### Scene 2 – The Hidden Keys
The vault cannot initially be opened, so the group follows the clues on the note.

[[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] searches:
- The kitchen, where he finds a large key hidden behind a fake rock under a table
- The suite, where he finds a second key

While searching the suite, he also uncovers a secret door leading to the vault.

Inside, the group finds the stolen **5 pounds of Residium**.

---

### Scene 3 – Return to the Tower
After identifying the items they recovered from the hideout, the group parts ways with [[Gwen]] and begins the journey back to [[Slaterock Tower]].

When they arrive, they smell food being prepared by [[Hotesh]].

They meet with [[Eskyld]] and show him the recovered Residium. He is pleased with their success and confirms that only one ingredient remains: the **Divine Tear**, believed to be in [[Shadebarrow]].

[[Eskyld]] explains that [[Shadebarrow]] was once a fortress of the betrayer gods, and that those who go there do not seem to return.
