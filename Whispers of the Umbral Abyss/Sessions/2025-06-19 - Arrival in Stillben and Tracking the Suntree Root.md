# 2025-06-19 - Arrival in Stillben and Tracking the Suntree Root

# To do
![![Campains/Whispers of the Umbral Abyss/Quest log .md]]

## Recap 


## Summary 
- The group arrived in Stillben, noting the poor quality of buildings, high crime rate, and the general state of disrepair.
- They established a base at The Sable Note tavern and gathered information on local markets from the tavern keeper, Tassa.
- Kaelos engaged with local beggars by offering them copper coins, while the group visited various shops, including The Dust Vein Curio.
- C.H.A.R.L.E.S made a deal with the shopkeeper Esree to investigate local thieves in exchange for a discount on gear upgrades.
- Roland used a location spell to track the Suntree Root to Greydawn Hall, a mansion in a wealthy district known for illegal auctions.


## Story 

The group arrives at [[Stillben]] after tracking through the swamp. In the city the group noticeses that a lot of the buildings are built of wood and does not seem like good quality. Holes in the ground are spottily repaired and there is high humidity and a faint stench of rot from the swamp in the air. There seems to be a lot of crime in the city. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] asks the nearest person in the city after a place to eat and is pointed towards [[The Sable Note]](Tavern). Inside they get something to eat and order a room as well. They sit down to discuss what the plan is in the city. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] speaks with the tavern keeper [[Tassa]] and is informed that he can buy and sell things in [[The Dust Vein Curio]] or he can go to [[The Emberpit forge]] or [[The Breakwater Goods and Sundries]]. 

During lunch [[Roland]] says that he might have a way to track the [[Suntree Root]] but they decide to do that during the evening and spend the day going through the cities markets. Going through the city [[Kaelos]] stops to give some copper coins to each of the beggars. They first go to [[The Dust Vein Curio]], where they meet an elderly Tiefling woman who is sitting behind the counter. She has a lot of rings on all her fingers. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] makes a deal with the woman to find a group of people stealing from others in the city in order to get half price off an item that could give more firepower to his gun. [[Kaelos]] introduces himself to the Tiefling and her name is [[Esree]] and tries to sell some of their goods and he gets a deal from her. [[Roland]] cast a location spell to find the [[Suntree Root]] and gets a ping in the northwestern part of the city. They leave the store and goes to that part of [[Stillben]], here they find a much nicer neighborhood with mansions and large homes. They get pinged in front of a mansion with a sign that says [[Greydawn Hall]], on the way to [[The Emberpit forge]] [[Kaelos]] stops by one of the beggars to get some information about the [[Greydawn Hall]] and he says that it is a place where you can get things at an auction if you have the right connections and an invitation as the things are not legal. 

At [[The Emberpit forge]] they see an assortment of weapons. They go back to the tavern at evening time to get some food at discuss a plan in the privacy of the room.
