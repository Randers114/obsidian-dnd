## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – Tallstack Farm
[[Kaelos]] and [[Thaldrin]] travel from the city to [[Tallstack Farm]], noting a heavy and somber mood lingering after the recent attack.

At the farm, they find signs of damage and clear evidence that large livestock has been dragged away. They meet [[Mira]], who explains that the farm was attacked two nights ago during the night. The attackers took all livestock but showed no interest in harming the family.

Her husband, [[Joren]], works to repair the damage while their daughter, [[Elsa]], helps.

Inside the stables, the group observes signs of panic among the animals before they were taken. However, outside there are no signs of struggle—only dragging marks leading away from the farm, all pointing north.

The group discovers markings on the stable walls resembling the symbol seen on attackers in the city. [[Mira]] claims these were not present before the attack but appeared afterward. [[Joren]] confirms the same pattern at other locations where animals were taken.

---

### Scene 2 – Into the Forest
Investigating further, the group identifies two sets of tracks leaving the farm: one obvious and one deliberately concealed.

They choose to follow the hidden tracks into the forest. Along the way, they are ambushed by a pack of wolves and manage to defeat them.

Continuing onward, the trail leads them deeper into the forest.

---

### Scene 3 – The Chapel
The group discovers an old chapel guarded by four individuals. After defeating the guards, they enter the structure.

Inside, they encounter a woman and kill her. Upon searching her belongings, they find a letter addressed to [[Mavrik]], suggesting a connection to the wider events.
