## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The First Prism
The group continues solving puzzles within the first portal. After completing the sequence, a golden dome opens, revealing a floating prism reflecting light.

[[Roland]] retrieves the prism, and the group returns to the office within [[Slaterock Tower]].

---

### Scene 2 – The Gravity Cube
They enter the third portal, emerging into a strange cube where gravity shifts across each surface. The sky beyond the windows is filled with stars and constellations.

They are attacked by incorporeal undead, identified by [[Roland]] as a wraith and a specter. After defeating them, they discover that gravity can shift between surfaces, allowing movement across the cube.

They find another portal and move through it.

---

### Scene 3 – The Bone Staircase
Beyond the portal lies a winding staircase formed from bone, with porcelain masks embedded in the walls.

As [[Roland]] casts healing magic, two gargoyles animate and attack.

During the fight, [[Thaldrin]] attempts to assist by leaping between the bone steps. He loses his footing and falls, disappearing into the depths below.

The group continues downward, retrieving another prism before returning to the tower.

---

### Scene 4 – Pressing On
Despite the loss of [[Thaldrin]], [[Kaelos]] and [[Roland]] choose to continue.

They enter another portal, arriving in a humid crypt filled with a putrid smell.

---

### Scene 5 – The Maze of Illusions
Within the crypt, they navigate a maze-like structure:
- Briefly encountering copies of themselves
- Discovering objects placed on pedestals (emerald, amber, ruby)

They encounter magical barriers:
- Illusion-based walls
- Transmutation effects

At one such wall, the structure itself reveals itself as a mimic and attacks.

After defeating it, they continue deeper into the maze.
