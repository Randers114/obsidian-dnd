## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – Sesaly Returned
The group helps [[Sesaly]] down from the tree and begins the journey back. On the way down, they find her other shoe.

They reach [[Whitestone]] late in the evening and go directly to [[Aldra's House]]. [[Aldra]] is overjoyed to see her daughter returned safely. She puts [[Sesaly]] to bed, then returns to thank the group for bringing her home.

---

### Scene 2 – Better Standing in Whitestone
After leaving [[Aldra]]’s home, the group goes to inform [[Thassian]] that [[Sesaly]] has been found.

[[Thassian]] tells them that this success should improve their standing with the city leadership and that he may be able to arrange a meeting the next day regarding Residium.

The group then returns to [[The Sun's Delight]].

---

### Scene 3 – Night in the City
That evening, the group splits up for personal errands:

- [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] searches the city for firearms and finds [[Gilmore’s Glorious Goods]], though no guns are available there
- [[Roland]] visits a large temple to [[The Dawnfather]] and prays
- [[Kaelos]] looks for incense to resummon [[Nibs]], but the shops are already closed

They all return to the inn to rest.

---

### Scene 4 – Roland’s Dream
During the night, [[Roland]] receives a dream-vision from [[The Dawnfather]].

In the dream, he asks for guidance against [[The Writhing Warden]]. [[The Dawnfather]] speaks of [[The Writhing Warden|Nefyracsus]]], an elder demon lord of the Abyss who steals the life force and essence of other demons. He explains that long ago [[The Writhing Warden|Nefyracsus]] was imprisoned in an [[Affyrimental]] to keep him bound forever.

[[The Dawnfather]] warns that the actions of various factions may lead to his release. He tells [[Roland]] that he trusts him to make the right choice when the time comes, and says he would not have allowed him to find his shield otherwise.

---

### Scene 5 – Guns and Goods
The next morning, [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] questions guards carrying firearms and learns more about Whitestone’s special rifle corps:

- It is a prestigious unit
- It trains under the lord of [[Whitestone]]
- [[Lord Darolo]] is the inventor of guns in this realm
- The corps has only 30 members

Meanwhile, [[Roland]] and [[Kaelos]] visit [[Gilmore’s Glorious Goods]]:
- [[Kaelos]] buys incense
- [[Roland]] looks at magical items, including:
  - A Bag of Holding
  - A returning-weapon belt

Both items are too expensive for the group.

---

### Scene 6 – Audience with Lord Darolo
The group meets [[Thassian]] by the [[Suntree]], and he leads them to the castle.

Inside, they notice portraits of a noble family, including one with a Tiefling among them. They are brought to a large study where [[Lord Darolo]] receives them.

He shows immediate interest in [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]]’ construction and firearms.

[[Roland]] explains that the group returned a portion of the [[Suntree Root]] after taking some for their own purpose. To build trust, he casts a zone of truth over the group.

[[Lord Darolo]] quickly asks whether they were sent by [[Eskyld]], implying he has heard of such efforts before.

After discussion, he retrieves 4 pounds of Residium from his desk and tosses it to [[Roland]], saying it is a reward for the group’s service in recovering [[Sesaly]].

At the end of the meeting, [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] challenges [[Lord Darolo]] to a shooting contest.
