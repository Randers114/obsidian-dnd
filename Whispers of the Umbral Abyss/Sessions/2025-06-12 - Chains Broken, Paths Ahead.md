## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The Ringmaster’s Orders
After clearing the camp, the group searches the leader’s tent and finds valuables and a letter.

[[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] reads it aloud:
- Orders from [[Midnight Managerie]]
- Instructions to capture exotic races
- Reference to a “showcase”
- Signed by [[Valerya Noir]], the Ringmaster

---

### Scene 2 – The Last of the Camp
The group continues through the cave:
- Frees remaining prisoners
- Finds Orc living quarters
- Discovers shrine to [[Gruumsh The Ruiner]]

They recover a ring from the shrine but little else of value.

---

### Scene 3 – The Cost of Survival
The group escorts prisoners back toward [[Sephrah]].

During the journey:
- They become lost and must camp
- Several captives die overnight from injuries
- They improvise a way to carry the dead

---

### Scene 4 – Return to Sephrah
They reach the monastery and hand over survivors and the deceased.

Meeting [[Leada]]:
- She thanks them but offers limited reward
- Provides new task:
  - Investigate elemental activity
  - Related to [[Plane of Air]]
  - Directed to [[Maeve]]

---

### Scene 5 – Moving Forward
The group stays at [[The Breezy Plateau]] and meets [[Tariic]].

They decide:
- Prioritize mission to [[Stillben]]
- Return later for the elemental issue

They travel onward, defeat a swamp creature, and arrive at [[Stillben]].
