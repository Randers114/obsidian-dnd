# 2026-03-19 - The Discovery of Residium and the Path to Shadebarrow

## To do

## Recap 


## Summary 
The party defeats a hideout leader, recovers residium, learns about the Myriad department, and discovers the next objective: the Divine Tear in Shadebarrow, a dangerous fortress.

## Story 

After exploring the hideout they end up in one corner room where they find the leader of the hideout, she has two shadow mastiffs beside her and the group attacks her. After they win they search her body and find some platinum and a note with text: 
1. Kitchen 
2. Meeting room 
3. Suite 
They also search her office for items and find a lot of orders about crimes. [[Gwen]] tells the group that she is here on behalf of her family and helps the group because of her father. She also recognises the crest on the order to find the residium that [[Roland]] found and says it stands for the [[Myriad]] department in [[Imaan]]. The vault could not be opened but following the note [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] goes to the kitchen and found a big key behind a fake rock under a table. He then goes to the suite the leader stayed in and found a second key, in his investigation he also found a secret door leading to the vault. There the group finds the 5 pounds of residium. 

After identifying all the items they found in the hideout. They bid farewell to [[Gwen]]and start their travels back to the tower. After coming back to the tower they smell the food that [[Hotesh]] is preparing. They go talk to [[Eskyld]] and show him that they have found the residium that the group has found. He is happy that they found the residium, and says that now they are only missing the Divine tear that they think is in [[Shadebarrow]]. 

[[Eskyld]] explains that [[Shadebarrow]] is an old fortress for the betrayer gods and that people does not seem to return from it after going there.
