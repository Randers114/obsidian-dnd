## 📖 Summary
The group successfully ambushes [[Sister Grieva]] and her forces, reclaiming the [[Suntree Root]], though she escapes using teleportation magic. On their return journey, they are pursued by another hostile group tied to a mysterious faction. After dealing with the threat, the group returns to [[Sephrah]] and resolves the elemental disturbance by closing a rogue portal to the [[Plane of Air]].

## 📚 Story (Detailed Narrative)

### Scene 1 – The Ambush
The group executes their plan to ambush [[Sister Grieva]].

- [[Hotesh]] is used as bait
- [[Sister Grieva]] arrives with backup, suggesting she anticipated betrayal

A battle breaks out:
- The group defeats her forces
- They successfully reclaim the [[Suntree Root]]

However:
- [[Sister Grieva]] escapes using a teleportation spell

---

### Scene 2 – Something in the Swamp
The group begins their journey back toward [[Sephrah]].

While traveling:
- [[Roland]] hears movement in the swamp
- Something is following them

At camp:
- [[Roland]] confronts the pursuers
- They demand the [[Suntree Root]]

Negotiation fails, and combat begins.

---

### Scene 3 – The Second Threat
After defeating the group, they find:

- Gold
- A badge marked with the symbol of [[Luxon]]
- A letter:
  - Contains threats
  - Orders them to retrieve the [[Suntree Root]]
  - Signed only as **“D”**

---

### Scene 4 – Return to Sephrah
The group reaches [[Sephrah]] and seeks out [[Maeve]].

She confirms:
- The elemental threat remains active
- A rogue portal to the [[Plane of Air]] must be closed

---

### Scene 5 – Closing the Rift
The group accompanies [[Maeve]] to the portal.

Plan:
- [[Maeve]] closes the portal
- The group distracts and defeats a large elemental

Outcome:
- The elemental is defeated
- The portal is successfully closed
