# Summary 
*   **Preparation in [[Westruun]]:** The group stocked up on magic items at Gilmore’s Glorious Goods. They noted a heavy, pressured atmosphere in the city, with increased guards serving the new mayor.
*   **Infiltration of [[Shadebarrow]]:** Upon reaching the fortress, the party scouted the area and defeated five members of The Ravagers, a cult worshipping the Betrayer Gods.
*   **Entering the Fortress:** The main doors featured depictions of torture and symbols of the Betrayer Gods. Inside, Roland used his status as a follower of the gods to activate a defaced symbol of Avandra, which opened the way forward.
*   **Combat and Exploration:** After defeating several blood-like creatures, the group discovered a room containing a skull-shaped vase and inscriptions in Abyssal. 
*   **The Sacrifice:** Kaelos translated the text, which referenced "oblivion" (Tharizdun) and demanded the relinquishing of hope and valued items. Roland sacrificed his childhood holy symbol by placing it in the vase; the item turned to dust, triggering a secret wall to open.

# Story 

After resting up at talking to [[Eskyld]] the group goes into the city [[Westruun]] to stock up for the coming excursion to [[Shadebarrow]]. Going to the city they find it harder to enter the city as the city guard is checking all people trying to enter, the mood in the city is a bit pressured as there are more guards in the city. Some of the guards are carrying the symbol of the new mayor. They head straight for [[Gilmore’s Glorious Goods]], After stocking up on magic items the group moves out towards [[Shadebarrow]]. 

After arriving to [[Shadebarrow]]they see a massive stone wall surrounding a fortress, [[Kaelos]] sends his familiar [[Nibs]] over the wall to scout it out. He sees 5 enemies inside the walls, after sneaking inside [[Roland]] recognises them to be part of [[The Ravagers]] who is a group that worships one of the [[Betrayer Gods]]. They attack the ravagers and kill the ravagers. They move to the door of the fortress, there is a giant door to the fortress with depictions of all kinds of torture. On it is also symbols for the 9 [[Betrayer Gods]] and [[Roland]] recognizes the symbol for [[Gruumsh]], [[Vecna]] and [[Asmodeus]]. They push open the doors and enter a giant hall with 50 feet to the ceiling. In the end of the entry hall there is another large door with a symbol for [[Avandra]], the symbol is faded as someone has tried to remove the symbol by scratching it out. After [[Roland]] touches the symbol for [[Avandra]] the door lights up red and slides open, he gets the feeling that the doors open because he is a follower of the gods. As they move into the next room a couple of blobs slithers out, it looks like a blob of blood that has mouths all over the body, they attack the group. After beating the blobs, they move into the next room. In the next room they see a vase formed as a skull where it looks like it has contained lilies, impressive that they have hold this long even though they have withered. Along the top of the table there is some text inscribed that neither of the group can read. However [[Kaelos]] can cast a Comprehend Languages ritual. 

The text is in abyssal and reads: 
"Cast that which you value into oblivion"

[[Kaelos]] recognizes that oblivion in this context refers to [[Tharistun]]. He then moves to the wall to read the text there also in abyssal, it reads: 

"To me touch your hand,
To me relinquish hope
To you accept death
To you invite hollowness"

[[Roland]] takes off his necklace with a symbol to his god, he has had that since childhood and decides to put it in the vase. He drops it into the vase, after a few seconds the eye sockets of the skull vase flashes red, his necklace turns to dust and the wall behind the skull opens.
