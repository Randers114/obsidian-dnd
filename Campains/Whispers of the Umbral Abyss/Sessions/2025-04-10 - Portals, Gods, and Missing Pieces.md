## Recap 


## Summary 

- The group meets the Realmseer, [[Eskyld]], who explains that a failed experiment to open a portal to [[Sigil]]—a hub of the multiverse—scattered him into molecules.
- [[Kaelos]] learns that [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] is a being from the world of [[Eboron]] and part of the multiverse. [[Eskyld]] reveals he left the [[Arcana Pansophical]] due to ridicule over his quest to find an ancient flying city.
- [[Eskyld]] lists three rare components needed to complete his experiment: **10 pounds of residuum** (from [[Whitestone]]), **a root of the Suntree** (in [[Stillben]]), and **a divine tear** (possibly in [[Shadebarrow]]).
- [[Roland]] is troubled that the required components, like the divine tear and the Suntree root, are sacred to his god, [[The Dawnfather]].
- The group decides to retrieve the Suntree root first and returns to [[Westruun]] to buy healing potions and an immovable rod at [[Gilmore’s Glorious Goods]].
- [[Eskyld]] teleports them to [[Sephrah]], where they speak with monks at a monastery and learn that more Genasi have been kidnapped by a criminal group higher up in the mountains.
## Story 
The man says that he is the Realmseer and that his name is [[Eskyld]], [[Roland]] goes over to [[Eskyld]] to heal him to soothe the pain he just went through. He says that the experiment he was performing went wrong and that he was split into molecules. [[Eskyld]] snaps his fingers and [[Jekt]] picks something up, and reveals that he is invisible to the group. [[Eskyld]] reveals that [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] is from another world, and that he looks like a warforged from the planet [[Eboron]] and that it is part of the multiverse. [[Eskyld]] also says that his experiment was that he was trying to open a portal to [[Siegel]] which is a center point of the multiverse with people from all realms. [[Eskyld]] also says that his goal is finding another of the flying cities from ancient times and that he has been ridiculed by his previous organization for the pursuit. His previous organization was [[Arcana Pansophical]] where he was a Maker. He separated himself from the organization because of the ridicule and came out on his own to find his goal. 

[[Jekt]] the unseen servant starts to cook which looks weird to the group because he is invisible. He is a conjured servant from the Realmseer. [[Kaelos]] asks about the things [[Eskyld]] is missing for his experiment and he reveals that he is missing 3 things to complete it. He list the things by least controversy. 

- 10 punds of residium 
	- This can be found in [[WhiteStone]], and can maybe be bough, bit of a controlled substance. 
- Small root of the Suntree
	- The root can be found with thieves in [[Stillben]]
- A divine tear
	- The divine tear can be found in [[Shadebarrow]]

He also says that there are thieves in [[Stillben]] that has gotten ahold of some roots from the Suntree. [[Roland]] is a bit upset that the things needed are a symbol of his God [[The Dawnfather]]. He also says that the divine tear can maybe be located in [[Shadebarrow]]. After the food and talks the group goes to one of the empty rooms to rest. 

In the morning they wake up to the smell of bacon and eggs. They go out and eat breakfast, then they talk to [[Eskyld]] about the mission that they need to go through. [[Kaelos]] asks [[Eskyld]] about the Aether in their possession but he does not know much about it. But he says that the Aether is high level alchemic necromantic potion. 

The group decides to set out for [[Stillben]] first to find the thieves and get back the root of the Suntree first. [[Eskyld]] can teleport the group to [[Sephrah]] which is about a days travel from [[Stillben]]. Before going however they decide to go back to [[Westruun]] and buy some potions for the coming trip. They go to [[Gilmores Glorious Goods]] to buy the potions. They buy 4 healing potions and an immovable rod. They go back to the tower to find [[Eskyld]] and start their trip to [[Stillben]] by teleporting them to [[Sephrah]]. They arrive in front of a monastery inside a pavilion with runes in the bottom. Up the mountainside the see a city built into it. 

They stop in the monastery and ask for directions for [[Stillben]]. During the talk they find that some Genasies has been kidnapped and it seems to be a criminal organization that is behind the abductions. They have heard that they have been taken longer up into the mountains.
