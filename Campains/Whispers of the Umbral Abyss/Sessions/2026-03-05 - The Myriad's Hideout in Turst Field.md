# 2026-03-05 - The Myriad's Hideout in Turst Field

## To do
![![Campains/Whispers of the Umbral Abyss/Quest log .md]]

## Recap 


## Summary 
After C.H.A.R.L.E.S.'s gun is tempered, the party travels to Turst Field, a city with integrated gnolls, to recover stolen Residium from the Myriad. They meet Gwen, an independent agent, who helps them enter the Myriad's hideout via the cistern, leading to a confrontation with bandits.

## Story 
After the shooting contest where [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] lost, [[Percival]] still offered [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] to temper his gun. [[Percival]] leads them inside to a workshop in the basement of the castle. He offers to grant more distance or more power to the gun, [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] chooses to have the distance enhanced. After the tempering the group goes back to the inn and starts the travel back to [[Westruun]], on the way they will go through [[Turst Field]]. After a day of travelling they reach the city [[Turst Field]]. 

In the city they notice that apart from the others they have visited, there is a number of #gnolls in the city who are incorporated. The shipment of Risidium in the city was stolen by the [[Myriad]] and held at their hideout here, they were told by [[Thassian]] to find a person named [[Gwen]] as she is keeping an eye on the shipment and the [[Myriad]]. The group also has some blueprints gotten from [[Thassian]] over the hideout where the Residium is being held. [[Roland]] starts the search for [[Gwen]] by going to the inn and asking about [[Gwen]] in the inn. The owner of the inn does not know anything about anyone named [[Gwen]] but he would ask his wife. The group goes to their room to look at the blueprint of the hideout and hatches a plan to split up and scope out the part of the city where it should be in order to find it. [[Kaelos]] asks the owner about the entrance to the cistern, he says that the official entrance is at the bank but closed off, he also says that they could go east of the city and then maybe climb down to the cistern. The group goes east and try to find a way down. They find a steep cliff with a hole down to the cistern and they climb down to the hole and enter. 

They walk back to the city through the cistern [[Roland]] hears some footsteps and they try to follow the sound, but it is hard to follow through the echoes of the cistern. After walking around the maze in the cistern they find the entrance to the hideout, a marble door without handles block the path. After checking for enchantments they get interrupted by [[Gwen]]. She says that she just used a rope to climb down. [[Gwen]] tells the group that she is an independent third party and is interested in the Risidium not falling into the wrong hands. [[Gwen]] says that the door needs a passphrase and says it out loud, the door slides open. 

The group accompanied by [[Gwen]] enters the hideout. A fight breaks out with some of the bandits.
