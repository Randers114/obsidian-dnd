## Summary 
- [[Kaelos]] and [[Thaldrin]] travel from the city to [[Tallstack Farm]], noting the somber mood in the city after a recent attack.
- [[Tallstack Farm]] is visibly ravaged, with signs that large livestock has been dragged away.
- A family—[[Mira]], her husband [[Joren]], and daughter [[Elsa]]—is seen cleaning up the damaged farm.
- The attack occurred two nights ago, targeting only the livestock while sparing the humans.
- Inside the stables, evidence shows that the animals panicked before being dragged away outside.
- Dragging marks found outside the stables point in a northward direction.
- A distinctive mark on the stable wall resembles the pin found on a cultist in the city.
- [[Mira]] explains she hadn’t noticed the mark before the attack; similar markings appear where animals were taken from the fields.
- [[Joren]] mentions that these markings were not present on the day of the attack but appeared the following morning.
- The group finds two sets of tracks—one obvious and one hidden—and following the hidden tracks into the forest leads to a wolf attack and, eventually, an encounter at an old chapel with four guards, where they defeat the guards, kill a woman, and discover a letter addressed to [[Mavrik]].
## Story 

[[Kaelos]] and [[Thaldrin]] moves from the city to [[Tallstack Farm]] in the city they notice a pressed mood as the attack affected the entire city. The group notices that the farm is a bit ravaged and it seems like some large livestock has been dragged away. They notice a man walking with his daughter and wife cleaning up the farm. The woman informs the group that they were attacked two nights ago were the livestock was taken. They came in the night and took all the livestock. The attackers did not seem interested in the humans on the farm at all but only took all the animals. Her name is [[Mira]]. Her husband is repairing the farm his name is [[Joren]] and her daughter [[Elsa]] is helping as well.  Inside the stables it was clear that the goal of the attack was to steal the animals. It seems like the animals were frantic with steps inside the cages but outside there seemed to be no resistance and only dragging marks. All the dragging marks outside the stables and other places at the farm points north. The group find a mark on the stables wall that has similarities to the pin found on the cultist in the city. [[Mira]] did not notice the mark before the attack but she says that the same markings are where the other animals were taken from the fields. [[Joren]] informs the group that the markings they found was not present the day of the attack but first the morning after. 

The group finds that there are two sets of tracks leading out from the farm, one obvious and one they have tried to hide. The group follows the hidden tracks into the forest, in the forest a pack of wolves find the group and attack. After the wolf attack the group follows the tracks and find an old chapel with 4 guards outside. They defeat the guards and move inside the chapel where they find a woman and kills her. In her inner pocket they find a letter that is sent to [[Mavrik]].
