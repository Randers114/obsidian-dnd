## Story 
The man in the room drinks a canister after threatening the group that they would never leave. He was [[Zevrin]].

The group defeats him and searches him. Before the battle he mentioned the [[Sect of The Writhing Warden]]. On him he had an amulet with the symbol of the eye the group found at the old temple in the forest. The group takes the canisters he has left and leave the underground space. They walk towards [[Campains/Whispers of the Umbral Abyss/Organizations/Cobalt Soul]] in [[Westrun]]. At night the group rests and [[Kaelos]] is awoken early in the morning by an ice fiend standing in the fireplace, she reveals herself to be the patron of [[Kaelos]] and sends him on a task to retrieve concentrated Aether. 

The others wake up after the fiend leaves and they travel further towards [[Westrun]], on the road they meet a man who informs them that the city has chosen a new leader and there is a bit of discontent. The leader is the first time being a woman and it is [[Lady Cerafis Thaldrin]]. 

During the night [[Kaelos]] summons his familiar and tells him to scout ahead, the next day [[Kaelos]] tells the team that he has been contacted by his patron and it has given him information about where the kidnapped people are. They are held in [[Thortil]]. The group decides to continue to [[Campains/Whispers of the Umbral Abyss/Organizations/Cobalt Soul]] to find out more about the ritual the [[Sect of The Writhing Warden]] is performing in order to better stop it. 

The group arrives at the city [[Westrun]], and goes to the [[Campains/Whispers of the Umbral Abyss/Organizations/Cobalt Soul]]. Inside [[Westrun]] the group buys some missing travel necessities like tents, bedrolls and rope. Walking through more of the city the group finds a store called [[Gilmores Glorious Goods]]. Inside the store they meet Daisy the shop assistant who says that Gilmore i not present in the store.
