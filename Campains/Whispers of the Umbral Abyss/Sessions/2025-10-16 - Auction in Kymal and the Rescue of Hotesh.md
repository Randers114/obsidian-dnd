# 2025-10-16 - Auction in Kymal and the Rescue of Hotesh
# To do
![![Campains/Whispers of the Umbral Abyss/Quest log .md]]

## Recap 


## Summary 
- The party attended an auction in Kymal where they witnessed the sale of various items, including spices, an obsidian dagger, and pre-Calamity scrolls.
- Roland successfully bid 375 gold pieces to purchase a Loxodon slave named Hotesh, outbidding a priestess named Sister Grieva.
- Sister Grieva later purchased the Suntree Root for 1000 gold pieces.
- The group freed Hotesh and offered him a position at their mage tower.
- They devised and initiated an ambush against Sister Grieva in the woods to retrieve the Suntree Root.

## Story 

The auction begins and the first item up for action is some spices which gets sold for 73 gold pieces. [[C.H.A.R.L.E.S]] hears some chiseling in the back rooms. The next item up for auction is an obsidian dagger made from the south of [[Wildemount]] which is imbued with poison. The dagger gets sold for 317 gold pieces. The next item up for bid is is a chest full of pre-calamity scrolls. The scrolls gets sold for 795 gold pieces. [[Roland]] overhears a woman priest in the corner saying that the next item is a slave and she says that would be one more for their collection. 

The speaker says that the next item is a gift from the [[Midnight Managerie]] and is an exotic creature, that has been captured. It is a Loxodon. [[Roland]] starts the bidding with 250 gold. [[Roland]] buys the slave for 375 gold pieces in a bidding war against the woman. The next item on the action is a ring that will give the person wearing it the ability to walk on water. It gets sold for 130 gold. The next item is an old map of the continents and the bidding starts with 175 gold pieces. [[Roland]] hears someone trying to break in below and informs the speaker who says that they have it under control. 

The next item up for bid is the [[Suntree Root]] and says it is their head item. The item goes  for 1000 gold pieces to the woman from the bidding war with [[Roland]] for the slave. Her name is [[Sister Grieva]]. [[Roland]] asks the speaker about where the slave is from and she says that he is from the [[Midnight Managerie]] and their main base is in [[Emon]]. The Loxodons name is [[Hotesh]]. The groups walks back to the inn with [[Hotesh]], they tell him that he is now free, but they want to offer him a job with them back at the mage tower. They hatch a plan to get the root from [[Sister Grieva]] by pretending that [[Hotesh]] is still a slave and ambushing them in the woods. The next day they set up the ambush in the woods and the fighting begins.
