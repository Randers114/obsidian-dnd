## Story 

The group has tied up the vampire in the basement and have a conversation with her about the organizations she works for, but she does not know anything. The group decides to kill her before moving on. The group kills the vampire. They decide to search the mansion for items where they find 2 spells scrolls. The group leaves the city and decides to head back towards [[Kymal]]. They head to the guard station to talk to [[Brynja Ironbrow]], She recommends that we talk to the [[Campains/Call of the Netherdeep/Organizations/Cobalt Soul]] about the cannister of Aether and what to use them for. She also recommends that we go to [[Zephrah]] to talk to the Air Ashari. 

The group prepares and heads for [[Westrun]] to find the [[Campains/Whispers of the Umbral Abyss/Organizations/Cobalt Soul]]. During the trip [[Thaldrin]] tells about his desire for new shoes, his old ones were taken from him by someone who held him captive. Ever since them he has not been able to find shoes that fits his feet well, they all hurt after a short while. On the trail [[Roland]] lets the others know that he hears steps outside of the path. The group is attacked by some ravagers. They go to see Archivist [[Lorvein Drenner]] of the [[Campains/Whispers of the Umbral Abyss/Organizations/Cobalt Soul]] to tell her about the encounter with the vampire. She tells the group that the vampires will ring alarm bells up north around [[WhiteStone]]. [[Lorvein Drenner]] performs a ritual on the cannister of Aether and does not get much information about it, but she tells that if ingested people get stronger at the cost of life force. She also says that in high enough quantities it can be used to free and draw demon lords to this realm.
