## Story 
After escorting the captives outside the city the group sets up camp and rests for the night. The next morning they set out for the city again to try and stop the cultists. On the way back to the city the group notices that there is a lot more silent in the city than it has been in the last few days that they were in the city. They find their way back to the mansion, all the corpses of the cultists that they fought on their way inside has been dragged away and they follow the trail to where they were taken. They find another estate were things seemed to have been barricaded. They find an open door and the trail ends inside the estate. Behind a statue inside the estate they find a secret corridor with some stairs down to a basement, here they sere more signs of bodies being dragged inside. They see a woman standing inside a room, she tells that this is her experiment and she is creating [[Aether]], a battle breaks out.  
After beating her and the cultists the group ties her up and interrogates her. She informs her that she is here on orders by [[Theran]]. She says that she is a part of [[The Umbral Abyss]] which is an organization of vampires. 

[[Kaelos]] calls on his patron and gives her the [[Aether]], her name is [[Malvoria]]. Her domain in [[Cania]] which is the ninth layer of hell.
