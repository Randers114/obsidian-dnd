# 2025-07-10 - The Singer's Request and the Customs Ledger

# To do
![![Campains/Whispers of the Umbral Abyss/Quest log .md]]

## Recap 


## Summary 
- The party returned to Stillben after defeating the druid in the forest.
- They visited the tavern to trade their loot and get food.
- They consulted the singer about the ledger they were seeking.
- The singer clarified that the ledger they possessed was not the official one, but contained mentions of The Myriad and The Clasp.
- The singer tasking the party with finding the actual customs captain's ledger from the docks.


## Story 

After defeating the druid in the forest the group returns to [[Stillben]], they go to the tavern to set off the packs they took and get some food. They talk to the singer who sent them to the waterfront watch to get the ledger. She informs them that it is not the official ledger they are looking for but another book that has mentions of [[The Myriad]] and [[The Clasp]]. After eating at the tavern they return to the waterfront.
