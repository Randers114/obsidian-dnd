## Recap 


## Summary 

- The group discovers that Genasi are being kidnapped and [[Kaelos]] recognizes the [[Midnight Managerie]] insignia from his past.
- They meet [[Leada]], who confirms the insignia and says she can be reached via the guards at [[Sephrah]].
- Following the kidnappers' trail into the mountains, they find a [[Midnight Managerie]] camp guarded by orcs and free a captive lizardman.
- Inside a cave in the camp, they rescue more captives and defeat the gang’s enforcers in battle.


## Story 

The group overhears someone talking about some kidnappings of the Genashi and [[Kaelos]] recognizes an ensignia she is holding as part of the [[Midnight Managerie]] that held him captured. [[Leada]], she says we can reach her through the guards at [[Sephrah]] and she will be in the castle. The group asks the monk where the people where the people were taken and where they found the insignia. 

They follow the trail of the kidnappers and reach a camp further up the mountain. There are Orc guards and this seems like the home of the [[Midnight Managerie]] gang. They invade the camp kill some of the external guards who also have the [[Midnight Managerie]] insignia and free a captive lizardman outside. They proceed into a cave in the camp that goes into the mountain. Inside they find a lot of captives which they free and proceed to do battle with the enforcers. They win and kill all of the criminals.
