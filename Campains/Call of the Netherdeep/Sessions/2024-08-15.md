## Story 

A fight starts in the fort when the group is discovered. After defeating the bandits the group frees the prisoners, and gets told that more bandits are under a trapdoor with more prisoners. Under the trapdoor there is a long latter that leads down below the fort. The group finds 3 bandits and the fighting begins. After defeating the thugs the group moves further into the cave and finds a small camp. They see a caged loxodon and the 2 leaders of the bandits while they are butchering some of the exotic races.
