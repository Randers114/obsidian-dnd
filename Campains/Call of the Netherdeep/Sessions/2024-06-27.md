## Story 

[[Jaez]] comes back from being a sheep and tells that he has some magic that can surge at different times that can transform him into different animals. [[Jaez]] gets the jewels from the now dead Moorbounder carpet. Moving around the ground level in the [[Hythanos Estate]] the group finds another dead guardsman in the kitchen. The group enters a large room and there are statues of hooded figures pointing into the room filled with display cases. In one side of the room there is a sarcophagus with intertwining snakes. In the first display case is a gold necklace with a gold amulet, it has a placket displaying "Found on the second level". 

On one wall there are large stone murals depicting some history scenes. One of them are i a large beast with horns which is the horned king [[Beafometh]] who is a demon. 

The next display case a silver sword, with a placket saying it was recovered in the temple 100 years ago. [[Tsaryah]] recognizes the sword as one made by her people. [[Jaez]] tries to destroy the glass to the silver sword. The hooded statue points at [[Jaez]] and attacks him. [[Tempa]] takes out the sword from the display case and the two hooded statues comes to life and attacks the group.

The group defeats the to constructs and goes back to examining the room. [[Jaez]] breaks the glass to the gold necklace and lifts it out of the display case. One of the already broken display cases reads "Adamantite Mace" and is empty. The next display case has a small pouch of white powder. The placket reads "Powder of sneezing and choking". The next large display case holds a staff with a placket reading "The staff twisted with the shadows of the [[Umbre Gates]]". The case in the back of the room, the glass is broken and a statue is inside, "Signet of the Children of Malice, worn by Drider servant of the [[Spider Queen]]"

Drider er bloodsworn Drow and where so loyal they mutated into half spiders. The signet gives of negative magic. The last display case holds a whip formed by a long strand of muscle with teeth at the end. The placket reads "This whip was recovered by a cultist of [[Shahir]]". At one end of the room the sarcophagus is not able to be opened. 

The group moves back and goes up the stairs of the [[Hythanos Estate]]. Upstairs the group finds another dead guard, this one is a finer dressed than the other guards downstairs. Inside the master bedroom [[Tempa]] moves across the room and is hit by a lightning trap and the group are attacked by a demon looking thing that was hiding in the corner. [[Tsaryah]] says that the telescope lightning trap could have been a glyph of warding. Inside a guestroom the group finds two knapsacks with the mark of [[Campains/Call of the Netherdeep/Organizations/Cobalt Soul]] those could be from the two people the group was looking for from [[Question]]. [[Question]] told the group that they got here a couple of days ago but all the evidence points to them being here for longer. In the next room of the Estate there are a lot of broken things, several workbenches are splintered and the room smells of soot and death. Inside the room the group are attacked by a zombie like monster crawling on all fours.
