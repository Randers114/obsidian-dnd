## Story 

The monster was wearing [[Tenarin Hythanos]] (male drow) skin. The team defeats the monster, and goes back to looking around the mansion. The monster was a [[Dybbok]], a creature that posses dead peoples bodies wearing their skin. Inside the last room in the [[Hythanos Estate]] the team finds the key to the chest in the other room and some sketches of some rooms, one of them with an alter, and another one with some red dots and a statue. The sketches could be things from [[Betrayers Rise]]. [[Jaez]] informs the group that the [[Hythanos]] den is usually above board in their dealings with different matters. Inside the room [[Tempa]] finds 3 vials of acid and takes them into his bag of holding. In the other room the key finds a chest with where the key they found on [[Tenarin Hythanos]] opens it, and inside the chest the group finds some gold, platinum and healing potions. The group goes back to the barracks to talk to [[Verin Thelis]] and tell him about the dead guards inside the [[Hythanos Estate]]. [[Verin Thelis]] is shocked about the dead guards and the death of [[Tenarin Hythanos]]. For informing the city guard the team receives 100 gold. The team goes back to [[The Ready Room]] to talk to [[Question]] about her companions. The group informs her that inside the [[Hythanos Estate]] along with the dead guards. [[Jaez]] pulls out the sketches from [[Tenarin Hythanos]] but [[Question]] can't tell what they are about. [[Jaez]] also shows [[Question]] some notes they found but it was written in code and she cannot read. [[Question]] is also shown the Signet of [[Children of Malice]] and the says the the red spot inside is Ruidium. It seems like [[Aloysia]] is looking at the group while they are talking to [[Question]]. The team takes [[Question]] to their room to go away from onlookers. She says that all of the artifacts found in the [[Hythanos Estate]] was cursed. She also gives the team some gold for investigating what happened to her friends, the team will still keep a lookout for them. 

The group takes out the items they found to investigate what they do [[Jaez]] looks at the staff of decay and [[Tempa]] looks at the Golden Goggles that they found at the [[Crematorium]]. The goggles could use identify, and [[Tempa]] uses them on the Whip, he finds out that the whip is a cursed Whip of the Serpent's Fang. The group grabs something to eat and goes to bed early to get some rest. The next day they decide to investigate the [[Tascaren Lading company]] which could be a front for the crime group [[Myriad]]. The group finds a large storage facility with no people around. [[Jaez]] can hear some footsteps inside and the group decides to look for another entrance. By looking in a window the group can see that inside there are a lot of crates and it looks like a warehouse. [[Jaez]] picks the lock on the window and sneaks inside with invisibility inside he sees 5 people and one of them is a female Tabaxi. Inside an office [[Jaez]] finds some documents written in code that seems like [[Myriad]] writing. The people inside hears [[Jaez]] and start scurrying about inside the building. 

They discover [[Jaez]] then [[Tempa]] and [[Tsaryah]] rushes in through the window and a fight ensues. After killing the other four the leader, the Tabaxi female drops her weapons and yells her surrender.
