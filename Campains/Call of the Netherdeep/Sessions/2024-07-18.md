## Story 

The place seems to be a front for [[Myriad]]. The female Tabaxi leader informs the group that they stole the crates from the [[The Seventh Clasp]] because they have gotten a task to steal all the things sent to the [[Vermillion Dream]] organization. She says there is a red stone in the creates. [[Tempa]] goes into a storeroom and takes the crates from the room out into the main room with the others. [[Tempa]] finds 10 bottles of purple liquid in the back room and it is some sort of drug, also 3 bags of powder drugs. [[Jaez]] kills the Tabaxi bandit leader. In one end of the warehouse there is a bulletin board with a bounty on [[Jaez]]. Contact person on the sheet is [[Caelen Duskwalker]]. [[Jaez]] says he did not know that things would go so far, the bounty on [[Jaez]] is 2000 gold. He is connected to one of the dens in [[Resona]] and they are the ones putting out the bounty. He was in the den against a fee so he is in a lot of debt to the den. [[Caelen Duskwalker]]  was the friend of [[Jaez]] and he did not think that he would put the bounty on him. [[Jaez]] uses a magic disguise because of the bounty. 

The group travels outside the city, down towards the place where they heard about the people capturing the exotic races. The group opens the lockbox and find a bag of crimson gems inside. The lockbox is what [[Aloysia]] was looking for and it seems to be the same material as the Signet of the [[Children of Malice]] is made of. After resting in the wilderness [[Tempa]] uses his googles to identify the red gems and find out that it is **Ruidium** and it is a material that corrupts things and is used to enchant things. 

[[Jaez]] reads [[Teneren Hythanos]] notes and he finds out that it is possible to close the portal to the abyss and cleanse the [[Betrayers Rise]] temple by destroying [[Arms of the Betrayers]]. He also reads in the [[Myriad]] notes that they should intercept all shipments of **Ruidium** before it is sent to [[Ank'harel]], the letter is signet by [[Verys El Sharan]]. 

After a few days the group arrives at a fort. The walls are around 20 foot high and there are watchtowers that rise above the walls. After climbing the walls while [[Tempa]] is a spider and the others are invisible. They discover multiple people inside with weapons and some races that are in cages.
