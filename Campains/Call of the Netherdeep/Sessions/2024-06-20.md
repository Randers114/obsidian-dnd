## Story 

Talking to [[Prolyx]] the group explains the visions. He does not recognize the name [[Alyxian]] He mentions a cursed area east of the city. That could hold a shrine. The group moves back to the inn. The red elven woman from [[Vermillion Dream]] talks to the group and tries to hire them to find a crate. She says it was stolen from a shop. The shop name was [[The seventh clasp]]. [[Aloysia]] is the name of the elven woman from [[Vermillion Dream]]. The group moves over to the teethling woman sitting in the corner from [[Campains/Call of the Netherdeep/Organizations/Cobalt Soul]]. She informs the group that history will repeat itself again with the fight with demons happening around the temple. The teethling from [[Campains/Call of the Netherdeep/Organizations/Cobalt Soul]] is named [[Question]]. She shows the team two drawings, one a drawing of where the team found the amulet and another about the amulet itself with more pieces. She informs that the drawings are based on her research about the history of the [[Betrayers Rise]] Temple. She also says that the amulet was an important focal point around the calamity. She shows a rubbing of a long haired woman looking into the distance. She points the group to a collector called [[Teneren Hythanos]], he might have more information. She has sent to colleagues to [[Teneren Hythanos]], which was [[Uraya]] and [[Israel]]. 

The group moves to the [[The Seventh Clasp]] to investigate what happened to **[[Aloysia]]'s** property. **Jaez** finds a symbol in the warehouse of a wagon wheel. The wheel is a symbol of the [[Tascaran lading Company]]. The company is rumored to be a front of the [[Myriad]], which is an organized crime syndicate. The [[Tascaran lading Company]] is in the south part of the city. 

The group moves over to the [[Hythanos Estate]]. The place seems dark and no one is answering when knocking on the door. Through the window the team can see something that looks like blood on the floor. Inside **Jaez** finds a book that explains that the gates to [[Betrayers Rise]] are called the [[Umbre gates]] and they lead to different configurations of the Temple. There is also a body of a city guard and the body seems to be a couple of weeks old. [[Jaez]] tried to steal from a Moorbounder carpet and it comes to life to attack the group.
