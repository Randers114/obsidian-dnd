## Moon Circle 
- **Combat Wild Shape**: You can use your Wild Shape as a bonus action and as a way to heal yourself by expending spell slots. This makes you incredibly versatile and resilient in combat.
- **Circle Forms**: Your Wild Shape options include more powerful creatures than those available to other druids. At 2nd level, you can transform into a beast with a challenge rating as high as 1. This capability increases with your druid level, allowing you to take on the forms of even more powerful beasts and, eventually, elementals.
- **Primal Strike**: At 6th level, your attacks in beast form count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage, which is crucial in fighting certain creatures.
- **Elemental Wild Shape**: Starting at 10th level, you can expend two uses of Wild Shape at the same time to transform into an air, earth, fire, or water elemental, offering significant combat and utility benefits.

Within the heart of the forest in **Savalirwood**, where the ancient trees weave stories in the whisper of their leaves, **Tempa**, the Loxodon druid, was raised under the guiding light of the moon. With no family to speak of the nature became the nearest family along with the druids in the small grove. **Tempa** grew up with **Kavi Gentletrunk** the only other Loxodon in the grove, along with their small band of friends: **Liriel Moonshadow**, **Theren Whisperwind**, **Faelen Greenleaf** and **Nimue Starbloom**. 

Their life in the grove was not just one of quiet contemplation and guardianship but also of constant interaction with the vibrant life around them. **Tempa's** hands, though gentle, were always in motion—tending to plants, sculpting the earth, and occasionally, in moments of deep thought, fiddling with the tools and totems that hung from their woven belt. This absent-minded habit often led to the accidental destruction of some of their belongings, much to their chagrin and the amusement of their fellow druids.

**Tempa's** connection to nature was not just of the flesh and spirit but of the mind as well. They saw lessons in every dewdrop and every leaf, drawing wisdom from the silent dance of the natural world. This wisdom was a lantern guiding their path, illuminating the truths that lay hidden in the heart of the wild. For **Tempa**, the constructs of civilization were but fleeting shadows when compared to the eternal beauty and truth of nature. This belief was the bedrock of their ideals, shaping their every action and decision.

### Guide

When making a druid, consider why your character has such a close bond with nature. Perhaps your character lives in a society where the Old Faith still thrives, or was raised by a druid after being abandoned in the depths of a forest. Perhaps your character had a dramatic encounter with the spirits of nature, coming face to face with a giant eagle or dire wolf and surviving the experience. Maybe your character was born during an epic storm or a volcanic eruption, which was interpreted as a sign that becoming a druid was part of your character’s destiny.

Have you always been an adventurer as part of your druidic calling, or did you first spend time as a caretaker of a sacred grove or spring? Perhaps your homeland was befouled by evil, and you took up an adventuring life in hopes of finding a new home or purpose.

Druids of the Circle of the Moon are fierce guardians of the wilds. Their order gathers under the full moon to share news and trade warnings. They haunt the deepest parts of the wilderness, where they might go for weeks on end before crossing paths with another humanoid creature, let alone another druid.
